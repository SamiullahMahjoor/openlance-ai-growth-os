# Open Lance Acceptable Use Policy

This document owns Open Lance's enduring acceptable use policy: the durable governance of what conduct is permitted and prohibited on the platform. It is a legal policy in the Legal namespace and follows the Legal Document Standard defined in knowledge/legal/README.md. It instantiates the foundational legal principles of respect for applicable law, good faith and fair dealing, and respect for the rights of others, owned by knowledge/company/legal.md, and it conforms to them and never restates or weakens them. Where this policy and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This policy owns the durable acceptable-use policy only. It is not an enforcement procedure, it is not legal advice, and it names no jurisdiction's law.

# Purpose

This document owns one legal concern: the enduring policy governing acceptable conduct on Open Lance. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine the durable standard of permitted and prohibited use, distinct from how that standard is enforced, from the trust and safety mechanics that support it, and from the law of any jurisdiction.

# Principles

These are the enduring, domain-specific principles Open Lance holds for acceptable use. Each instantiates a foundational legal principle owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and applies it to this concern.

- Use is lawful. The platform is used only for lawful purposes, and never to do or enable what the law forbids.
- Use is honest and fair. Participants act in good faith and deal fairly, and do not deceive, defraud, or exploit others through the platform.
- Use respects others. Participants respect the rights, safety, and dignity of others, and do not harass, abuse, infringe, or endanger them.
- Use does not undermine the marketplace. Participants do not circumvent the protections the platform provides or misuse it in ways that undermine trust in the marketplace.
- Conduct has consequences. Prohibited conduct may lead to action against the participant, applied fairly and consistently, with the execution of that action owned by the process documents under knowledge/processes/.

# Requirements

These durable requirements follow from the principles above. They contain no jurisdiction-specific law and no implementation.

- Use of the platform is confined to lawful purposes, and unlawful use is prohibited.
- Fraud, deception, misrepresentation, and other bad-faith conduct are prohibited.
- Harassment, abuse, threats, infringement of others' rights, and conduct that endangers others' safety are prohibited.
- Circumventing the platform's protections, including conduct that moves an engagement off the platform to evade its safeguards, is prohibited where it undermines those safeguards.
- The permitted and prohibited conduct is stated so participants can understand it, and it is applied consistently, with enforcement executed through knowledge/processes/ and the trust and safety mechanics owned by knowledge/product/.

# Responsibilities

These responsibilities are assigned by role. The roles themselves are owned by knowledge/product/roles.md and are referenced here, not defined.

- Participants, in every role defined by knowledge/product/roles.md, are responsible for using the platform lawfully, honestly, fairly, and with respect for others.
- Open Lance is responsible for setting the acceptable-use standard clearly, for applying it consistently, and for acting on violations fairly.
- Accountable humans are responsible for enforcement decisions that carry legal significance, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.

# Boundaries

This document owns the acceptable-use policy only. It owns none of the following, and references each by its canonical owner.

- The foundational legal principles: knowledge/company/legal.md.
- The relationship framework and terms under which use occurs: knowledge/legal/terms.md.
- Enforcement execution, including moderation and dispute procedures: the process documents under knowledge/processes/.
- The trust and safety mechanics, states, and business rules that support enforcement: knowledge/product/.
- The produced acceptable-use instrument and its wording: the Output level.
- Applicable law and legal advice: an absolute external constraint and the province of accountable humans and qualified counsel; the specific requirements that any jurisdiction imposes are identified and interpreted by them, not by this policy.
