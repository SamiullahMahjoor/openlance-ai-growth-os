# Open Lance Campaign Strategy

This document owns the Campaign strategy for Open Lance: how it shapes and runs time-bound marketing initiatives. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy and framework only. Individual campaigns are time-bound initiatives at the Campaign level of the knowledge hierarchy and produced as Outputs, owned elsewhere. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic framework for Open Lance's campaigns: how a time-bound initiative is shaped, so that concentrated effort advances a specific goal without drifting off strategy.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is that when Open Lance concentrates effort into a time-bound push, it does so coherently, drawing on the same strategy as everything else, so campaigns compound the story rather than fragment it. This serves the growth lifecycle owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.

# Strategy

- A campaign is a time-bound, goal-focused initiative. It has a defined goal, audience, and window, and it advances a specific stage of the lifecycle owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md for a specific audience owned by knowledge/customers/.
- A campaign draws on the foundation, not on invention. Its message comes from the messaging framework owned by knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md, its value from knowledge/marketing/value-propositions.md, and its position from knowledge/marketing/positioning.md. A campaign never invents an off-strategy message.
- A campaign runs across the channel mix. It uses the channels owned by knowledge/marketing/channel-strategy.md and the channel documents, coordinated toward its goal.
- A campaign is measured against its goal. Its success is judged by whether it advanced its stated goal; the specific metrics and tools are operational.
- This owns the campaign framework, not the campaigns. How campaigns are shaped and run is owned here; each individual campaign is a Campaign-level initiative and an Output, owned elsewhere.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- The lifecycle and audiences a campaign advances: knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md and knowledge/customers/customers.md.
- The position, value, and message a campaign carries: knowledge/marketing/positioning.md, knowledge/marketing/value-propositions.md, and knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md.
- The channels a campaign runs across: knowledge/marketing/channel-strategy.md.

# Application

This strategy directs how any campaign is shaped: its goal, audience, message, and channel mix. Each individual campaign is planned and produced as an Output at the Campaign level, drawing on this framework. This document runs no specific campaign.

# Boundaries

This document owns the campaign strategy and framework only. It owns none of the following.

- Individual campaigns and their assets: the Campaign and Output levels of the knowledge hierarchy and the asset systems.
- The lifecycle and audiences: knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md and the Customers namespace, knowledge/customers/.
- The position, value, message, and differentiators: the foundation strategies under knowledge/marketing/.
- The channels: knowledge/marketing/channel-strategy.md and the channel documents.
- How messages are worded: the Brand namespace, knowledge/brand/.
- Analytics implementation and campaign tools: operational systems and the codebase.
