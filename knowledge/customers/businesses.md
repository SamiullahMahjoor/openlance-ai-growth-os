# Open Lance Businesses Segment

This document owns the Businesses segment for Open Lance: the demand side of the marketplace. It is a customer document within the Customers namespace, and it follows the Customer Document Standard defined in knowledge/customers/README.md. The identity of this segment in the inventory is owned by knowledge/customers/customers.md, and the Business role is owned by knowledge/product/roles.md; this document owns the segment profile only. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the durable understanding of who Open Lance's business customers are, what they need, how they behave, and how they speak. Its purpose is that every agent and contributor works from the same picture of the demand side, without holding any personal data and without restating what other documents own.

# Who They Are

The canonical identity of this segment is owned by knowledge/customers/customers.md. Within it, businesses range from a solo founder or an individual hiring help, through small teams, to established companies with recurring work. What unites them is the demand position: they define work, choose who does it, and pay for it. They tend to hold the Business role, owned by knowledge/product/roles.md.

The size and formality of a business varies, and that variation is a difference of degree within one segment, not a separate segment. Open Lance serves this range as a single demand-side audience today.

# Needs

Businesses come to Open Lance to satisfy the following durable needs. Each need is met by a product capability owned by knowledge/product/features.md; this document records the need, not the capability.

- Find capable, trustworthy talent for a defined piece of work, and be confident in that choice.
- Keep their money safe until work is delivered as agreed, rather than paying up front on trust.
- Hire without unfair or hidden costs weighing on the decision.
- Get a fair, predictable process from posting work to accepting it.
- Have a fair path to resolution when something goes wrong.
- Build and return to reliable working relationships over time.

The commercial terms behind these needs are owned by knowledge/product/pricing.md, and the mechanisms that satisfy them, such as escrow and reviews, are owned by knowledge/product/features.md and knowledge/product/entities.md. This document points to them and never restates them.

# Behaviors

Businesses tend to decide and act in the following durable patterns. The workflows and processes named here are owned by knowledge/product/workflows.md and the process documents under knowledge/processes/; this document records the behavior, not the workflow.

- They describe the work and make it available, then consider who responds, choosing on reputation and fit rather than the lowest price.
- They commit funds before work begins, review what is delivered, and release payment when it meets the agreed standard.
- They weigh trust signals, such as verification and past reviews, heavily in the choice.
- They prefer clarity and predictability, and they rehire people who did well rather than starting over each time.
- When a disagreement arises, they expect a fair, structured resolution rather than an unrecoverable loss.

# Language

Businesses describe their situation in the words of hiring and getting work done, not in platform terms. They talk about hiring, finding talent or freelancers, posting a job, getting a project done, staying on budget and on time, vetting candidates, reviewing a portfolio, and trusting who they pay. They frame success as work delivered well, on time, without being overcharged or let down.

This is the audience's own language, recorded so that communication can meet it. How Open Lance itself chooses words is a separate standard owned by knowledge/brand/vocabulary.md, and this document never restates it.
