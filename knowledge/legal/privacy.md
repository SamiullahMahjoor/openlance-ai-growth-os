# Open Lance Privacy Policy

This document owns Open Lance's enduring privacy policy: the durable governance of personal information. It is a legal policy in the Legal namespace and follows the Legal Document Standard defined in knowledge/legal/README.md. It instantiates the foundational legal principle of lawful handling of information and respect for user rights owned by knowledge/company/legal.md, and it conforms to that principle and never restates or weakens it. Where this policy and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This policy owns the durable privacy policy only. It is not the produced privacy notice, it is not legal advice, it names no jurisdiction's law, and it specifies no technical control.

# Purpose

This document owns one legal concern: the enduring policy governing how Open Lance handles the personal information participants entrust to it. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine the durable privacy policy, independent of the notice that expresses it, the systems that implement it, or the law of any jurisdiction.

# Principles

These are the enduring, domain-specific principles Open Lance holds for privacy. Each instantiates the foundational information-handling principle owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and applies it to this concern.

- Lawful and legitimate basis. Personal information is collected, used, and shared only where there is a lawful and legitimate basis for doing so.
- Purpose limitation. Information is used for the purposes for which it was collected and the participant was informed of, and not repurposed in ways they would not reasonably expect.
- Data minimization. Only the information genuinely needed for a legitimate purpose is collected.
- Respect for participant rights. Participants retain rights over their information, and Open Lance respects those rights and enables participants to exercise them.
- Transparency. How information is handled is disclosed honestly and understandably, so participants can make informed decisions.
- Protection. Personal information is protected against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access, with the specific safeguards owned by the systems that implement them.

# Requirements

These durable requirements follow from the principles above. They contain no jurisdiction-specific law and no implementation.

- Every collection, use, or sharing of personal information rests on a lawful and legitimate basis, and is confined to purposes the participant was informed of.
- Collection is limited to what a legitimate purpose requires, and information is not retained beyond what those purposes need, with the duration of retention owned by knowledge/legal/data-retention.md.
- Participants are able to understand what information is held about them and to exercise the rights they hold over it, including where they may access, correct, or request deletion of it.
- Where consent is the basis for a use of information, that consent is informed and is not assumed from silence.
- The handling of personal information is disclosed transparently, consistent with the disclosure policy owned by knowledge/legal/disclosures.md.
- Personal information is safeguarded, with the technical and organizational measures that safeguard it owned by the codebase and operational systems, not by this policy.

# Responsibilities

These responsibilities are assigned by role. The roles themselves are owned by knowledge/product/roles.md and are referenced here, not defined.

- Open Lance is responsible for handling personal information lawfully and transparently, for protecting it, and for enabling participants to exercise their rights over it.
- Participants, in every role defined by knowledge/product/roles.md, are responsible for the accuracy of the information they provide and for respecting the information of others they encounter through the platform.
- Accountable humans are responsible for decisions about personal information that carry legal significance, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.

# Boundaries

This document owns the privacy policy only. It owns none of the following, and references each by its canonical owner.

- The foundational information-handling principle: knowledge/company/legal.md.
- How long information is kept and when it is removed: knowledge/legal/data-retention.md.
- What Open Lance commits to disclose: knowledge/legal/disclosures.md.
- The produced privacy notice and its wording: the Output level.
- Security controls, storage, consent mechanisms, and other implementation: the codebase and operational systems.
- The operating entity and company identity: knowledge/company/company.md.
- Ownership of content and intellectual property: knowledge/legal/content-ownership.md and knowledge/legal/intellectual-property.md.
- Applicable law and legal advice: an absolute external constraint and the province of accountable humans and qualified counsel; the specific requirements that any jurisdiction imposes are identified and interpreted by them, not by this policy.
