# Purpose

This document owns a neutral, durable, factual description of Contra. Its purpose is that every agent and contributor works from the same accurate understanding of Contra, free of opinion or persuasion.

# Overview

Contra is a freelance platform organized around commission-free independent work. It presents freelancers as independents with portfolio-forward profiles and emphasizes direct relationships between freelancers and the clients who hire them.

# Marketplace Model

Contra operates on a commission-free model, in which freelancers keep the full amount a client pays for their work rather than surrendering a platform commission on it. Freelancers present portfolio-forward profiles, connect with clients directly, and manage projects and ongoing arrangements on the platform, which monetizes through means other than a commission on freelancer earnings. The specific current terms and monetization are external and change over time.

# Target Customers

Contra serves independent freelancers who want to keep their full earnings and build direct client relationships, and clients who prefer working with independents without a commission markup on the freelancer's pay.

# Strengths

Contra's durable strengths include its commission-free proposition for freelancers, a modern, portfolio-forward presentation of talent, an emphasis on direct client relationships, and an independent-first brand.

# Tradeoffs

Contra's model carries inherent tradeoffs. A newer platform has less scale and liquidity than long-established marketplaces, and monetizing without a commission on earnings shapes which services and protections the platform emphasizes.

# Where It Excels

Contra tends to serve best where an independent freelancer wants to keep full earnings and cultivate direct, ongoing client relationships, and where a client is comfortable working directly with independents.

# Relationship to Open Lance

Contra and Open Lance overlap in serving freelancers and clients. Open Lance's own emphasis, fee approach, and protections are owned by knowledge/company/ and knowledge/product/; this document describes Contra's. Their models differ in structure and in how each protects and monetizes the exchange. Open Lance's own model, including its fee and payment approach, is owned by knowledge/company/company.md and knowledge/product/, and how Open Lance positions itself relative to Contra is owned by the Marketing namespace. This document states the overlap and the difference as fact and argues no advantage.
