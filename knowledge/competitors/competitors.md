# Open Lance Competitor Inventory

This document is the single canonical inventory of the competitors that Open Lance recognizes. It records only that each competitor exists and what its identity is. It does not compare competitors, rank them, judge them, or describe their models in detail, and it does not define any Open Lance fact or positioning strategy. Those are owned elsewhere or, in the case of comparison and ranking, deliberately excluded.

This is a reference document within the Competitors namespace. It follows the namespace guide in knowledge/competitors/README.md, the repository constitution in knowledge/README.md, and the contribution process in knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md, and it does not restate the content of any document it references. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

Competitors in this inventory are recorded as durable identities. An entry continues to exist even as a competitor's current terms change. New competitors are added by extending this inventory; the structure of this document does not change as Open Lance grows.

# Purpose

This document owns the existence and identity of competitors. It is the single place where the presence of a competitor is recorded. No other document may add a competitor to the inventory, and other documents may only reference the competitors recorded here.

Its purpose is discovery and shared meaning: any human or AI contributor can find, in one place, which organizations Open Lance recognizes as competitors, and then follow a canonical reference to the document that owns each competitor's detail.

# Scope

This inventory lists every competitor Open Lance genuinely competes against and documents. Each competitor is represented exactly once and has exactly one canonical entry.

Each entry records identity only. It does not describe a competitor's model, strengths, tradeoffs, or relationship to Open Lance, which are owned by that competitor's own document, and it makes no comparison or ranking.

# Every Competitor Entry

Every competitor in this inventory uses the same thin structure. The structure records identity only.

- Name. The canonical name of the competitor.
- Description. A single neutral identity statement of what the organization is.
- Out of Scope. What this entry does not record, deferred to its owner.
- Related Knowledge. The canonical repository path to the document that owns the competitor's detail.

An entry contains no model detail, no strengths, no tradeoffs, no comparison, and no ranking. It records identity only.

# Definitions

These definitions are repository-wide and timeless. Each defers ownership where the concept is owned elsewhere.

- Competitor. The concept of a competitor is defined in knowledge/competitors/README.md. This inventory owns only the existence and identity of each competitor, not the definition of the term and not any competitor's detail.
- Competitor Group. A neutral, factual grouping of competitors used to organize this inventory by the kind of marketplace they operate. A group is an organizational convenience only; it carries no ranking, no judgment, and no authority.

# Architectural Identity

This inventory reinforces, and does not replace, the identity boundaries defined in knowledge/competitors/README.md.

A competitor entry represents only a durable identity of an organization Open Lance competes with. It never represents any of the following, each of which is owned elsewhere or excluded.

- A competitor's model, strengths, tradeoffs, or relationship to Open Lance. Owned by the competitor's own document under knowledge/competitors/.
- A comparison, ranking, or judgment. Excluded from this namespace.
- Competitive positioning strategy. Owned by the Marketing namespace, knowledge/marketing/.
- Any Open Lance identity, model, customer, or product fact. Owned by knowledge/company/, knowledge/product/, and knowledge/customers/.
- Volatile current terms, such as fees, features, or metrics. External sources, not the knowledge repository.

A competitor identity remains unchanged even as its current terms change. The identity of a competitor is independent of how it currently prices, packages, or operates.

# Ownership

- Each competitor has exactly one canonical entry, which lives in this document.
- No other document may add a competitor to the inventory or redefine a competitor's existence or identity.
- Other documents reference the competitors recorded here and never restate them.
- The detail of each competitor, its model, strengths, tradeoffs, and relationship to Open Lance, is owned by that competitor's own document under knowledge/competitors/.
- Competitive positioning strategy is owned by the Marketing namespace.
- Open Lance identity, product, and customer knowledge is owned by its namespace and is only referenced here.

# Reuse

Existing competitors are reused. A duplicate competitor is never created. When a contributor needs a competitor that already exists, they reference the existing identity recorded here rather than creating a new one. Before adding a competitor, a contributor confirms that no existing entry already records it, following the contribution process in knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md.

# Repository Growth

New competitors are added by extending this inventory. Each new competitor is a new entry under the appropriate group, using the same thin structure, and it points to the document that owns its detail. A competitor is added only when Open Lance genuinely competes against a distinct new organization, never because that organization is well known. The structure of this document does not change as the number of competitors grows, existing competitor identities never change, and growth is always additive.

# Repository Evolution Notes

These notes record worthwhile architectural observations only. They require no action now and describe no defect.

- Owning competitor documents. Each entry references a document under knowledge/competitors/ that owns the competitor's detail. Those documents are created alongside this inventory, following knowledge/competitors/README.md and knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md.
- Indirect competitors and substitutes. Beyond the direct freelance marketplaces listed here, Open Lance also faces indirect competitors, such as professional networks and vertical or niche platforms, and substitutes, such as direct hiring, staffing agencies, and off-platform working relationships. These are recognized here as categories and are added as named entries with their own documents only if one becomes a materially competitive organization, never speculatively.
- Grouping. Competitors are grouped by the neutral, factual kind of marketplace they operate, which is an organizational convenience and never a ranking. If Open Lance ever recognizes a competitor of a genuinely different kind, a new group is added without changing existing entries.
