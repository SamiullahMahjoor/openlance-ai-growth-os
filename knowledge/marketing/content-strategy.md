# Open Lance Content Strategy

This document owns the Content strategy for Open Lance: what content it creates and to what end. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy only. The produced content itself is an Output owned elsewhere, and how content is worded is owned by knowledge/brand/. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic decision about what content Open Lance creates and why. Its purpose is that content consistently educates and earns trust with both audiences, in service of the growth lifecycle.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is that content moves each audience from unaware to confident, teaching what matters to them and demonstrating that Open Lance is trustworthy and fair, so that content becomes a durable engine of acquisition and education. This serves the growth lifecycle owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.

# Strategy

- Create content that educates each audience on what matters to it, drawn from the needs owned by knowledge/customers/ and grounded in facts owned by knowledge/product/ and knowledge/company/.
- Serve the lifecycle. Awareness and education content carries acquire and educate; proof and decision content carries convert; help and depth content carries retain and grow.
- Carry the message. Every piece expresses the position and the messages owned by knowledge/marketing/positioning.md and knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md, constructed to the brand standards owned by knowledge/brand/.
- Be genuinely useful and honest. Content earns discovery and trust by being useful and true, never by manipulation or invented claims.
- Work with the other channels. Content is made to be found through search, owned by knowledge/marketing/seo.md, and distributed through social and email, so it compounds rather than sits idle.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- The audiences, their needs, and their language: knowledge/customers/customers.md.
- The messages content carries: knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md and knowledge/marketing/positioning.md.
- The standards for how content is worded and presented: the Brand namespace, knowledge/brand/.
- The facts content teaches: knowledge/product/ and knowledge/company/.

# Application

This strategy directs which content to create, for which audience and stage, and to what end. The produced content is an Output, created to the brand standards, made discoverable through the search strategy, and distributed through social and email. This document writes no article, page, or asset.

# Boundaries

This document owns the content strategy only. It owns none of the following.

- How content is worded, its voice, tone, and vocabulary: the Brand namespace, knowledge/brand/.
- The facts content teaches: knowledge/product/ and knowledge/company/.
- The audiences and their language: the Customers namespace, knowledge/customers/.
- How content is found in search: knowledge/marketing/seo.md.
- How content is distributed on social and by email: knowledge/marketing/social-media.md and knowledge/marketing/email-marketing.md.
- Produced content, and the content systems that store and publish it: the Output level, the asset systems, and the codebase.
