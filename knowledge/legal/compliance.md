# Open Lance Compliance Policy

This document owns Open Lance's enduring compliance policy: the durable commitment and approach to operating within applicable law. It is a legal policy in the Legal namespace and follows the Legal Document Standard defined in knowledge/legal/README.md. It instantiates the foundational legal principles of respect for applicable law and of human accountability for legal responsibility, owned by knowledge/company/legal.md, and it conforms to them and never restates or weakens them. Where this policy and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This policy owns the durable compliance policy only. It names no specific law, it is not legal advice, and it is jurisdiction-neutral.

# Purpose

This document owns one legal concern: the enduring policy governing how Open Lance commits to and approaches compliance with the law that applies to it. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine the durable compliance policy, distinct from any specific law or regulation, from the procedures that implement compliance, and from legal advice.

# Principles

These are the enduring, domain-specific principles Open Lance holds for compliance. Each instantiates a foundational legal principle owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and applies it to this concern.

- Lawful operation is foundational. Open Lance commits to operating within the law that applies to it wherever it operates, and treats lawful operation as a foundation, not an afterthought.
- Compliance is jurisdiction-neutral in policy. This policy commits to comply with whatever law applies, without naming or restating the law of any jurisdiction, so it remains valid as laws and jurisdictions change.
- Legal uncertainty is escalated. When the lawfulness of an action is uncertain, it is not taken until an accountable human confirms it, applying the principle of human accountability for legal responsibility.
- Compliance adapts. As the law that applies changes, Open Lance adapts to continue meeting its obligations.
- Cooperation with lawful authority. Open Lance cooperates with authorities where the law or a legitimate obligation requires, and does not conceal or obstruct.

# Requirements

These durable requirements follow from the principles above. They contain no jurisdiction-specific law and no implementation.

- Open Lance operates within the law that applies to it, and does not pursue any goal, policy, or automation that would require breaking it.
- Open Lance maintains a commitment to identify and meet the legal obligations that apply to its operation, and to adapt as those obligations change.
- Where the lawfulness of an action is uncertain, the action is escalated for human legal review through the process owned by knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md, and not taken until confirmed.
- The specific laws, regulations, jurisdiction-specific requirements, and the procedures that implement compliance are owned outside this policy, by accountable humans and counsel and by operational systems.

# Responsibilities

These responsibilities are assigned by role. The roles themselves are owned by knowledge/product/roles.md and are referenced here, not defined.

- Open Lance is responsible for operating lawfully, for maintaining its commitment to identify and meet applicable obligations, and for cooperating with lawful authority where required.
- Accountable humans are responsible for legally significant compliance decisions and for resolving legal uncertainty, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.
- Participants, in every role defined by knowledge/product/roles.md, are responsible for complying with the law and with the terms that govern their use of the platform.

# Boundaries

This document owns the compliance policy only. It owns none of the following, and references each by its canonical owner.

- The foundational applicable-law and human-accountability principles: knowledge/company/legal.md.
- The identification and interpretation of the specific laws and regulations that apply: accountable humans and qualified counsel. This policy owns the durable commitment and approach to complying with them, as directed by knowledge/company/legal.md, and does not restate the regulations themselves.
- The procedures, controls, and systems that implement compliance: operational systems and the codebase.
- The escalation process for legal uncertainty: knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md.
- Tax rules and financial obligations: the finance and legal owners outside this policy.
- Applicable law itself and legal advice: an absolute external constraint and the province of accountable humans and counsel.
