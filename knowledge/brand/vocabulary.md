# Open Lance Vocabulary Standard

This document owns the Vocabulary standard for Open Lance: how the organization chooses and uses words. It is a brand standard document within the Brand namespace, and it follows the Brand Document Standard defined in knowledge/brand/README.md. The identity of the Vocabulary concept is owned by knowledge/brand/brand.md; this document owns the standard only. It sets how words are chosen; it does not own the canonical names of the concepts words refer to, which are owned by their documents. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the standard for Open Lance's word choice. Its purpose is that the same idea is always said with the same clear, plain word, so that communication stays consistent and easy to understand for every reader and every AI agent.

# Standard

Open Lance chooses words by the following durable rules.

- Plain over clever. Prefer the common, everyday word to the technical, trendy, or invented one. If a specialist term is unavoidable, explain it in plain words the first time.
- One idea, one word. Use a single consistent term for a recurring idea rather than rotating synonyms. Consistency is clarity.
- Names follow the naming standard. Using the canonical name for anything the repository already names, rather than inventing an alternate word for it, is owned by knowledge/brand/naming.md. Word choice follows that standard and never restates it.
- Concrete over abstract. Prefer words that name a real thing or action over vague abstractions.
- Honest, never inflated. Avoid empty superlatives, buzzwords, and vague hype. Do not dress a claim in bigger words than the fact supports.
- Inclusive and respectful. Use words that include every reader and demean none. Avoid language that pressures, shames, or excludes.
- No manipulative language. Manufacturing urgency, scarcity, or fear to push a reader is a persuasion tactic owned by the messaging standard at knowledge/brand/messaging.md; word choice never introduces it.

# Rationale

Word choice is where the voice, owned by knowledge/brand/voice.md, becomes concrete, and where the organization's promise of a simple, transparent, and fair marketplace, owned by knowledge/company/mission.md and knowledge/company/principles.md, either holds or breaks. Plain, consistent words make the product simple to understand; honest words keep it transparent; inclusive words keep it fair. Rotating synonyms or reaching for jargon quietly erodes all three, so the standard fixes word choice deliberately.

# Application

The vocabulary standard applies to every outward communication in every medium. When writing, a writer or agent chooses the plainer word where a choice exists and uses one consistent term for a recurring idea, removing hype and jargon. This document states the rules of word choice; it does not hold the master list of product or role names, which live with their owners, and it never writes a specific piece of content, which is an Output.

# Boundaries

This document owns the vocabulary standard only. It owns none of the following.

- The identity of the Vocabulary concept in the brand inventory: knowledge/brand/brand.md.
- The voice that word choice expresses: knowledge/brand/voice.md.
- Situational tone: knowledge/brand/tone.md.
- How names are formed and written: knowledge/brand/naming.md.
- The canonical names of roles, entities, capabilities, states, and other concepts: their documents under knowledge/product/, knowledge/company/, and elsewhere in the repository.
- What the organization says it offers: knowledge/brand/messaging.md, and the facts owned by knowledge/company/ and knowledge/product/.
- Company identity, mission, and principles: the documents in knowledge/company/.
- Marketing strategy and campaigns: the Marketing namespace, knowledge/marketing/.
- Produced content, content systems, and tooling: the Output level, the asset systems, and the codebase.
