# Open Lance Growth Strategy

This document owns the Growth strategy for Open Lance: how it grows both sides of the marketplace across the customer lifecycle. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy only. The marketplace model and the customer segments it grows are owned by knowledge/company/company.md and knowledge/customers/ and are referenced, not restated. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic model for how Open Lance grows and the lifecycle it moves customers through. Its purpose is that every channel, campaign, and message is aimed at the right stage of a coherent, two-sided growth model.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is a marketplace that becomes and stays liquid, where both sides find what they need, and where customers move from first contact to lasting, expanding use. This serves the company's mission and marketplace model, owned by knowledge/company/company.md.

# Strategy

- Grow both sides in balance. A two-sided marketplace depends on both demand and supply, and neither side is grown so far ahead of the other that the experience degrades. The marketplace model is owned by knowledge/company/company.md; this strategy keeps its two sides, owned by knowledge/customers/, in balance.
- Move customers through five lifecycle stages: acquire, bringing an audience in; educate, building understanding and trust; convert, reaching a first hire or first job; retain, earning repeat use; and grow, expanding into more work and larger arrangements such as teams.
- Match the growth motion to each side. Businesses and freelancers enter, learn, convert, and expand differently, so the strategy applies the lifecycle to each side on its own terms, drawing on the segments owned by knowledge/customers/.
- Grow on trust, not inducement. Because the marketplace depends on trust and fairness, owned by knowledge/company/principles.md, every stage prioritizes earning trust over buying temporary activity.
- Own the lifecycle and the two-sided model, not the channels or campaigns that execute them. Which channels carry each stage is owned by knowledge/marketing/channel-strategy.md and the channel documents; which initiatives run is owned by knowledge/marketing/campaigns.md.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- The marketplace model and the principle of trust it grows on: knowledge/company/company.md and knowledge/company/principles.md.

- The value and messages each stage carries: knowledge/marketing/value-propositions.md and knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md.

# Application

The lifecycle is the frame every other execution strategy is aimed at. Channel, content, social, email, campaign, and launch strategies each serve one or more stages, for one or both sides. This document states the growth model in principle; it runs no channel or campaign, which are owned elsewhere and produced as Outputs.

This document owns the growth strategy and lifecycle only. It owns none of the following.
