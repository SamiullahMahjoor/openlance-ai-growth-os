# Open Lance Intellectual Property Policy

This document owns Open Lance's enduring intellectual property policy: the durable framework governing intellectual property on and around the platform. It is a legal policy in the Legal namespace and follows the Legal Document Standard defined in knowledge/legal/README.md. It instantiates the foundational legal principle of respecting intellectual property and the rights of others owned by knowledge/company/legal.md, and it conforms to that principle and never restates or weakens it. Where this policy and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This policy owns the durable intellectual property framework only. It is not a license instrument, it is not legal advice, and it names no jurisdiction's law.

# Purpose

This document owns one legal concern: the enduring policy framing how intellectual property is protected and respected in connection with Open Lance. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine the durable intellectual property policy, distinct from the specific ownership of user content and work product, from the brand marks, and from the law of any jurisdiction.

# Principles

These are the enduring, domain-specific principles Open Lance holds for intellectual property. Each instantiates the foundational intellectual property principle owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and applies it to this concern.

- Open Lance owns its own intellectual property. The platform, its brand identity, and the materials Open Lance creates are its intellectual property, and are protected.
- Others' intellectual property is respected. Open Lance does not infringe, misappropriate, or induce infringement of the intellectual property of participants or third parties.
- Rights are granted only as needed and clearly. Where Open Lance needs a right to operate the platform, that right is obtained through a clear, limited license rather than a claim of ownership.
- Infringement is not tolerated. Open Lance does not permit its platform to be used to infringe intellectual property, and it responds to credible claims of infringement.
- Participants keep their rights. Participants retain the intellectual property they bring to or create on the platform, except for the limited licenses they grant, with the ownership of their content and work product owned by knowledge/legal/content-ownership.md.

# Requirements

These durable requirements follow from the principles above. They contain no jurisdiction-specific law and no implementation.

- The intellectual property Open Lance owns, including its name and brand marks and the materials it creates, is protected, with the marks and brand identity themselves owned by the Brand and Company namespaces.
- Any right Open Lance takes in participant material is limited to what is genuinely needed to provide and operate the service, and is expressed as a license, not ownership.
- Participants warrant that they have the rights to the material they provide, and do not use the platform to infringe the intellectual property of others.
- Credible claims that material on the platform infringes intellectual property are addressed, and repeat infringement is not tolerated, with enforcement executed through the process documents under knowledge/processes/.
- The framework references, and does not restate, the ownership of user content and work product owned by knowledge/legal/content-ownership.md.

# Responsibilities

These responsibilities are assigned by role. The roles themselves are owned by knowledge/product/roles.md and are referenced here, not defined.

- Open Lance is responsible for protecting its own intellectual property, for respecting that of others, for taking only the limited licenses it needs, and for responding to credible infringement claims.
- Participants, in every role defined by knowledge/product/roles.md, are responsible for holding the rights to what they provide, for respecting others' intellectual property, and for the licenses they grant.
- Accountable humans are responsible for intellectual property decisions that carry legal significance, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.

# Boundaries

This document owns the intellectual property framework only. It owns none of the following, and references each by its canonical owner.

- The foundational intellectual property principle: knowledge/company/legal.md.
- Ownership of user-generated content and work product: knowledge/legal/content-ownership.md.
- The brand marks, logo, and brand identity: the Brand namespace and knowledge/company/company.md.
- The platform software and its implementation: the codebase.
- The produced license and assignment instruments and their wording: the Output level.
- Enforcement execution against infringement: the process documents under knowledge/processes/.
- Applicable law and legal advice: an absolute external constraint and the province of accountable humans and qualified counsel; the specific intellectual property requirements that any jurisdiction imposes are identified and interpreted by them, not by this policy.
