# Open Lance Launch Strategy

This document owns the Launch strategy for Open Lance: how it brings a new offering, capability, or the platform itself to market. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy and framework only. What is being launched is a product fact owned by knowledge/product/, and each individual launch is an Output owned elsewhere. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic framework for Open Lance's launches: how something new is introduced to market so that it is noticed, understood, and adopted.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is that when Open Lance introduces something new, the market understands what it is, why it matters, and why to adopt it, so that the new thing gains traction rather than passing unnoticed. This serves the acquire and convert stages of the lifecycle owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.

# Strategy

- A launch is the go-to-market for something new. It builds awareness of the new offering, explains its value drawn from knowledge/marketing/value-propositions.md and grounded in the capability owned by knowledge/product/, and drives first adoption.
- A launch is a focused kind of campaign. It uses the campaign framework owned by knowledge/marketing/campaigns.md, shaped around the specific goal of introducing and driving adoption of the new thing.
- A launch serves the relevant sides and stages. It addresses whichever sides of the marketplace the new thing serves, owned by knowledge/customers/, and concentrates on acquire and convert.
- A launch is honest about what is new. It represents the new offering truthfully, grounded in the facts owned by knowledge/product/ and knowledge/company/, and expresses the position owned by knowledge/marketing/positioning.md.
- This owns the launch framework, not the launches. How a launch is shaped and run is owned here; each individual launch is an Output, owned elsewhere.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- What is being launched: knowledge/product/features.md and knowledge/product/.
- The value and position a launch carries: knowledge/marketing/value-propositions.md and knowledge/marketing/positioning.md.
- The campaign framework a launch is shaped as: knowledge/marketing/campaigns.md.
- The audiences and lifecycle stages a launch serves: knowledge/customers/customers.md and knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.

# Application

This strategy directs how any launch is shaped: its goal, its value story, its audiences, and its adoption aim. Each individual launch is planned and produced as an Output, drawing on this framework and the campaign framework. This document runs no specific launch.

# Boundaries

This document owns the launch strategy and framework only. It owns none of the following.

- What is being launched, the offering or capability itself: the Product namespace, knowledge/product/.
- Individual launches and their assets: the Output level of the knowledge hierarchy and the asset systems.
- The general campaign framework a launch draws on: knowledge/marketing/campaigns.md.
- The value, position, and message a launch carries: the foundation strategies under knowledge/marketing/.
- The audiences and lifecycle: the Customers namespace, knowledge/customers/, and knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.
- How the launch is worded: the Brand namespace, knowledge/brand/.
- Launch tooling and analytics implementation: operational systems and the codebase.
