# Open Lance Social Media Strategy

This document owns the Social Media strategy for Open Lance: the role social channels play. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy only. Specific platforms, accounts, and tools are operational, and voice, tone, and imagery are owned by knowledge/brand/. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic decision about the role social channels play for Open Lance. Its purpose is that social presence consistently builds awareness, community, and trust with both audiences.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is that social channels make Open Lance visible and trusted where its audiences already gather, and turn attention into an ongoing relationship, rather than serving as a disconnected broadcast. This serves awareness and community in the growth lifecycle owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.

# Strategy

- Use social to build awareness, community, and trust, and to meet each audience where it gathers. The audiences are owned by knowledge/customers/; this strategy decides social's role in reaching them.
- Choose the role social plays, not a specific platform. This strategy owns the durable decision about what social is for, such as awareness, community, and distribution of content owned by knowledge/marketing/content-strategy.md. The specific platform, account, and tool are operational and live outside the knowledge repository.
- Carry the message and the brand consistently. Social expresses the position and messages owned by knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md, in the voice, tone, and imagery owned by knowledge/brand/, which it applies and never redefines.
- Engage as a relationship, not only a broadcast. Social listens and responds, treating attention as the start of a relationship, consistent with the fairness and honesty the brand commits to.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- The audiences and where they gather: knowledge/customers/customers.md.
- The messages social carries: knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md.
- The voice, tone, and imagery social applies: knowledge/brand/voice.md, knowledge/brand/tone.md, and knowledge/brand/imagery.md.
- The content social distributes: knowledge/marketing/content-strategy.md.

# Application

This strategy directs what social is for and how it carries the brand and message. The specific platforms, accounts, posting, and tools that implement it are operational. This document writes no post and manages no account.

# Boundaries

This document owns the social media strategy only. It owns none of the following.

- Voice, tone, and imagery: the Brand namespace, knowledge/brand/.
- The audiences: the Customers namespace, knowledge/customers/.
- The messages: knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md.
- The content distributed: knowledge/marketing/content-strategy.md.
- Specific platforms, accounts, schedules, and social tools: operational systems, not the knowledge repository.
- Produced posts and assets: the Output level and the asset systems.
