# Open Lance Mission

This is the authoritative home of the Open Lance mission. It defines what Open Lance does every day, for whom, and how, and it explains how the mission guides the daily decisions of humans and AI agents.

It complements two documents and does not repeat them. Company identity and positioning live in knowledge/company/company.md. The long-term destination and strategic direction live in knowledge/company/vision.md. This document covers only the mission and its use in daily execution. Where this document and a higher-authority document appear to differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Mission Statement

The following is the founder-approved canonical mission statement. It is immutable and may change only through an official amendment approved by the founder.

> Open Lance empowers businesses and freelancers through a transparent, secure, and fair marketplace with escrow-backed payments and tools that help both sides build long-term success.

Every section below interprets this statement. None of them may contradict it.

# Purpose of the Mission

The mission exists to make daily execution consistent. It answers what Open Lance does now, for whom, and how, so that every contributor, human or AI, works toward the same outcome in the same way.

The mission is distinct from the vision. The vision, defined in knowledge/company/vision.md, describes the long-term destination Open Lance is steering toward. The mission describes the daily work that moves the organization in that direction. The vision is the long-term test for a decision; the mission is the near-term test. A choice should pass both.

Operational decisions should align with the mission because daily choices compound. Consistent, mission-aligned execution is how the vision is reached, and small misaligned decisions accumulate into drift. Aligning to the mission keeps independent contributors and agents converging on the same result rather than diverging.

# Mission Principles

The mission implies a set of enduring operating principles. This section separates the principles stated directly in the mission statement from interpretations derived from it, so that no interpretation is mistaken for the founder-approved text. These are not new company values. The canonical company values live in knowledge/company/company.md, the canonical decision rules live in knowledge/company/principles.md, and formal definitions of terms such as trust and accessibility live in the Operational Definitions of knowledge/company/vision.md. This section explains only how each idea influences everyday work.

## Principles stated in the mission

These are drawn directly from the words of the mission statement.

- Fairness. The mission commits to a fair marketplace. Everyday work should treat businesses and freelancers even-handedly, and no choice should systematically advantage one side at the other's expense.
- Transparency. The mission commits to a transparent marketplace. Work should be explainable and free of hidden mechanics, and contributors should prefer clarity over concealment.
- Security. The mission commits to a secure marketplace with protected payments. Everyday work should protect participants' work, funds, and data by default, and should never trade safety for convenience.
- Long-term success. The mission explicitly aims at helping both sides build long-term success. Everyday work should favor durable outcomes for participants over short-lived wins.

## Derived interpretations

These are reasonable interpretations of the mission, not words in the canonical mission statement. They are labeled as derived so they are not mistaken for the official text. They are officially adopted as current operational guidance and remain binding until amended through the normal governance process defined in knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md. Each stays consistent with knowledge/company/company.md and knowledge/company/vision.md.

- Trust (derived). Fairness, transparency, and security together build trust, which knowledge/company/vision.md identifies as the marketplace's core asset. Everyday work should strengthen trust and never spend it for a short-term gain.
- Accessibility (derived from "empowers businesses and freelancers"). Empowering both sides implies a commitment to accessibility, which is defined in the Operational Definitions of knowledge/company/vision.md. The mission-specific reading is that empowerment is not achieved if accessibility is not upheld.
- Opportunity (derived from "empowers"). Empowering both sides is read here as expanding what participants can achieve rather than narrowing it.

When these principles come into tension with one another, they are resolved by the guidance referenced in the Daily Decision Framework below, not by this document.

# Daily Decision Framework

Contributors can apply the mission to a specific choice by asking the following. If a choice fails one of these tests, it should be reconsidered, and if the tension cannot be resolved, it should be escalated per knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md.

- Does this improve fairness for both businesses and freelancers?
- Does this increase transparency, or does it hide something?
- Does this strengthen trust, or does it spend trust for a short-term result?
- Does this protect the security of participants' work, funds, and data?
- Does this keep the marketplace accessible to participants of every size and location?
- Does this create opportunity for participants rather than reduce it?
- Does this benefit both sides, rather than one side at the other's expense?
- Does this support long-term success rather than a short-term gain?

This framework is a near-term filter. When it alone does not resolve a difficult decision, for example when fairness to both sides or the protection of trust pulls in two directions, contributors must apply the decision precedence and trade-off guidance defined in knowledge/company/vision.md. That guidance is not duplicated here.

# Applying the Mission

The mission shapes every function through enduring patterns rather than specific tactics.

- Product. Build so that fairness, transparency, and security are defaults rather than options, and so that both sides benefit from any capability.
- Marketing. Represent Open Lance accurately. Attract both sides with honest, verifiable claims, and never overstate or mislead to win attention.
- Sales. Set expectations honestly and earn trust rather than only converting. Serve the interests of both sides in any engagement.
- Customer Support. Resolve issues fairly and transparently, and treat every interaction as an opportunity to protect trust.
- Community. Maintain a fair and safe environment for both sides, and recognize genuine contribution rather than manipulation.
- Partnerships. Choose and maintain partners whose conduct upholds fairness, security, and trust, and never compromise participant interests for a partnership.
- AI Agents. Treat the mission as the near-term test for every output, keep both sides in view, and halt and escalate when a request would require violating the mission.

# What the Mission Does NOT Mean

The mission is a standard, not a blank check. It is commonly misread in the following ways.

- It does not require accepting every feature request. The mission is a filter for what to build, not a mandate to build whatever is asked.
- It does not justify sacrificing trust, fairness, or security for growth. Growth pursued at the cost of these is a violation of the mission, not an expression of it.
- It does not override legal obligations. Legal constraints outrank operational choices, as defined by the knowledge hierarchy in knowledge/README.md.
- It does not mean favoring one side to satisfy it. Fairness to both businesses and freelancers is required, even when one side would prefer otherwise.
- It does not license overstating, omitting, or inventing facts in order to appear mission-aligned. Honesty is part of transparency.
- It does not define commercial terms. Anything the mission implies about value to participants is bounded by the documents named in Out of Scope.

# Mission Checklist for AI Agents

An AI agent should confirm the following before producing work. If any answer is no or unclear, the agent halts and escalates per knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md rather than proceeding.

- The output is consistent with the canonical mission statement above.
- It serves businesses and freelancers fairly, and does not advantage one side at the other's expense.
- It is transparent and contains no misleading, overstated, or invented claims.
- It protects participant trust and security.
- It keeps the marketplace accessible and creates opportunity rather than reducing it.
- It favors long-term success over a short-term gain.
- It stays within this document's scope and pulls pricing, metrics, legal, roadmap, and campaign specifics from their own documents.
- It is consistent with knowledge/company/company.md and knowledge/company/vision.md.
