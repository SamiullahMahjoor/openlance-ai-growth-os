# Open Lance Channel Strategy

This document owns the Channel strategy for Open Lance: which classes of channel it uses and how they combine. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the portfolio strategy only. Each individual channel's own strategy is owned by that channel's document, and specific platforms and tools live in operational systems. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic decision about the channel portfolio: which classes of channel Open Lance invests in and how they work together. Its purpose is that channels reinforce one another toward the growth lifecycle rather than operating in isolation.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is to reach each audience where it already is, with a coherent mix in which each channel plays a distinct role and hands off to the next, so that marketing effort compounds. This serves the growth lifecycle owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.

# Strategy

- Select channels by audience and by lifecycle fit. A channel is invested in because an audience owned by knowledge/customers/ is reachable through it and because it fits a stage of the lifecycle owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.
- Give each channel a distinct role and let them hand off. Search and content carry discovery and education, social carries awareness and community, and email carries nurture and retention, so a customer can move from one to the next. Each channel's own strategy is owned by its document: knowledge/marketing/content-strategy.md, knowledge/marketing/seo.md, knowledge/marketing/social-media.md, and knowledge/marketing/email-marketing.md.
- Choose classes of channel, not specific platforms or tools. This strategy owns the durable decision to invest in a class of channel; the specific platform, tool, or account is operational and lives outside the knowledge repository.
- Combine channels so they reinforce a single story. Every channel draws its message from the messaging framework owned by knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md, so the mix tells one story.
- Allocate and reallocate by evidence. Where a channel serves the lifecycle well, it is invested in; where it does not, it is reduced, judged by outcomes rather than fashion.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- The lifecycle the channels serve: knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.
- The audiences and where they gather: knowledge/customers/customers.md.
- The message the channels carry: knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md.

# Application

This strategy directs how the individual channel strategies combine and hand off across the lifecycle. Each channel document owns its own channel; this document owns only the portfolio and orchestration. It selects no platform and runs no channel, which are operational and produced as Outputs.

# Boundaries

This document owns the channel portfolio strategy only. It owns none of the following.

- Each individual channel's own strategy: knowledge/marketing/content-strategy.md, knowledge/marketing/seo.md, knowledge/marketing/social-media.md, and knowledge/marketing/email-marketing.md.
- The lifecycle the channels serve: knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.
- The audiences: the Customers namespace, knowledge/customers/.
- The message: knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md.
- Specific platforms, accounts, tools, and analytics implementation: the operational systems and the codebase.
- Produced content and campaigns: the Output level and the asset systems.
