# Open Lance Content Ownership Policy

This document owns Open Lance's enduring content ownership policy: the durable governance of who owns content and work product created through the platform. It is a legal policy in the Legal namespace and follows the Legal Document Standard defined in knowledge/legal/README.md. It instantiates the foundational legal principles of respecting intellectual property and the rights of others, and of good faith and fair dealing, owned by knowledge/company/legal.md, and it conforms to them and never restates or weakens them. Where this policy and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This policy owns the durable ownership policy only. It is not a license or assignment instrument, it is not legal advice, and it names no jurisdiction's law.

# Purpose

This document owns one legal concern: the enduring policy governing ownership of the content and work product that participants create through Open Lance. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine the durable ownership policy, distinct from the broader intellectual property framework, from the mechanics of engagements, and from the law of any jurisdiction.

# Principles

These are the enduring, domain-specific principles Open Lance holds for content ownership. Each instantiates a foundational legal principle owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and applies it to this concern.

- Creators own what they create. Participants own the content and material they create, subject only to the licenses they grant and the ownership terms of an engagement they enter.
- Work product ownership follows the engagement. Ownership of deliverables produced in an engagement passes between the parties as their engagement terms provide, fairly and clearly.
- The platform does not claim ownership of user work. Open Lance takes no ownership of the content or work product participants create, and holds only the limited license it needs to operate the service.
- Ownership is clear before work begins. The parties to an engagement can know how ownership of the work product is to be handled before the work is done, so ownership is not left in doubt.

# Requirements

These durable requirements follow from the principles above. They contain no jurisdiction-specific law and no implementation.

- User-generated content, including profiles, listings, messages, and other material a participant creates, is owned by the participant who created it, subject to the licenses they grant.
- Ownership of deliverables and work product produced in an engagement follows the ownership terms the parties to that engagement agree, with the engagement and contract mechanics owned by knowledge/product/ and knowledge/processes/.
- Open Lance's own license in participant content is limited to what is genuinely needed to host, display, operate, and improve the service, and never amounts to a claim of ownership.
- The ownership terms that apply to an engagement are made available to its parties before the work product is created, so ownership is clear in advance.
- This policy references, and does not restate, the intellectual property framework owned by knowledge/legal/intellectual-property.md.

# Responsibilities

These responsibilities are assigned by role. The roles themselves are owned by knowledge/product/roles.md and are referenced here, not defined.

- Open Lance is responsible for not claiming ownership of participant content and work product, for limiting its license to what it needs, and for making ownership terms available before work begins.
- The parties to an engagement, in the roles defined by knowledge/product/roles.md, are responsible for agreeing the ownership terms of their work product and for honoring them.
- Participants are responsible for warranting that they own or have the rights to the content and work product they provide.
- Accountable humans are responsible for ownership decisions that carry legal significance, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.

# Boundaries

This document owns the content ownership policy only. It owns none of the following, and references each by its canonical owner.

- The foundational intellectual property and fair-dealing principles: knowledge/company/legal.md.
- The intellectual property framework, including the platform's own intellectual property and licensing: knowledge/legal/intellectual-property.md.
- The engagement, contract, and deliverable mechanics: knowledge/product/ and knowledge/processes/.
- The produced license and assignment instruments and their wording: the Output level.
- Applicable law and legal advice: an absolute external constraint and the province of accountable humans and qualified counsel; the specific ownership requirements that any jurisdiction imposes are identified and interpreted by them, not by this policy.
