# Purpose

This document owns a neutral, durable, factual description of Guru. Its purpose is that every agent and contributor works from the same accurate understanding of Guru, free of opinion or persuasion.

# Overview

Guru is a freelance marketplace organized around posted work, freelancer quotes, and escrow-protected payment. Businesses describe work, freelancers quote to perform it, and the two collaborate through a shared workspace.

# Marketplace Model

Guru operates as a quote-based marketplace with built-in payment protection. Businesses post work and review quotes from freelancers, the two coordinate through a shared workroom, and payment is held in escrow and released against agreed terms, with flexible schedules such as milestone or task-based payment. Membership tiers affect how many quotes a freelancer can send and the fees that apply. The specific current fees and terms are external and change over time.

# Target Customers

Guru serves small and medium businesses seeking freelancers, often for technical and ongoing work, and a base of freelancers who value escrow protection and flexible payment arrangements.

# Strengths

Guru's durable strengths include built-in escrow with flexible payment schedules, a shared workroom for collaboration, and fee arrangements that some freelancers find favorable relative to larger marketplaces.

# Tradeoffs

Guru's model carries inherent tradeoffs. Membership tiers gate how many quotes a freelancer can send, and the platform operates at a smaller scale than the largest general marketplaces.

# Where It Excels

Guru tends to serve best for small and medium business engagements, particularly technical or ongoing work, where escrow protection and flexible payment schedules matter to both sides.

# Relationship to Open Lance

Guru and Open Lance overlap in serving businesses and freelancers. Open Lance's own payment protection is owned by knowledge/product/; this document describes Guru's escrow model. Their models differ in how work is quoted and in fee structure. Open Lance's own model, including how it protects payment, is owned by knowledge/company/company.md and knowledge/product/, and how Open Lance positions itself relative to Guru is owned by the Marketing namespace. This document states the overlap and the difference as fact and argues no advantage.
