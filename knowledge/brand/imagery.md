# Open Lance Imagery Standard

This document owns the Imagery standard for Open Lance: the kind of images and visual style the brand uses, and the honesty, inclusion, and accessibility they must keep. It is a brand standard document within the Brand namespace, and it follows the Brand Document Standard defined in knowledge/brand/README.md. This document owns the standard only. The specific image assets, icon sets, files, and rendering are owned by the asset systems and codebase, and the palette imagery works within is owned by knowledge/brand/color.md. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the standard for Open Lance's imagery. Its purpose is that every image reinforces trust, fairness, and clarity, and never misrepresents or excludes, whoever or whatever selects or creates it.

# Standard

Open Lance uses imagery by the following durable rules.

- Authentic and human. Imagery shows real people doing real work, and the true range of people the marketplace serves. It avoids staged, clichéd, or generic stock that rings false.
- Clean and uncluttered. The visual style is light and calm, working within the brand palette owned by knowledge/brand/color.md. Imagery supports the message and never buries it.
- Simple, consistent iconography. Icons are simple, line-based, and consistent in weight and style. An icon clarifies meaning; it is not decoration for its own sake.
- Purposeful motion. Movement is restrained and earns its place by guiding attention or giving feedback. It is never gratuitous, and it always yields to a reader's reduced-motion preference.
- Honest. Imagery never misrepresents the product, its outcomes, the people who use it, or the results they can expect. What is shown is true.
- Inclusive. Imagery represents the breadth of people the marketplace serves and excludes none.
- Accessible. Meaningful imagery carries a text alternative, essential information is never conveyed by an image alone, and motion respects reduced-motion settings.

# Rationale

Imagery is a claim made without words, so it is held to the same honesty as words. Showing real, diverse people doing real work expresses the fairness and inclusion the organization commits to in knowledge/company/principles.md, and refusing to misrepresent outcomes expresses the transparency at the center of its mission, owned by knowledge/company/mission.md. A clean, calm style and restrained, respectful motion keep the experience trustworthy and unmanipulative, which is what a marketplace handling people's work and money owes them.

# Application

The imagery standard applies wherever images, icons, or motion appear, in every medium: screens, print, presentations, social, and documentation. When choosing or creating imagery, a contributor or agent selects authentic and inclusive images, keeps the style clean and within palette, uses motion only with purpose and with reduced-motion respected, and confirms honesty and accessibility. This document states the standard; the actual image assets, icon libraries, and rendering are owned by the asset systems and codebase, and it never itself produces an Output.

This document owns the imagery standard only. It owns none of the following.

- Typography set within or alongside imagery: knowledge/brand/typography.md.
- The logo: knowledge/brand/logo.md.
