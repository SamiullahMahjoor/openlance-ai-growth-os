# Open Lance Legal Principles

This document defines the enduring legal principles that govern Open Lance. It establishes the legal constraints and legal philosophy that every future policy, process, contributor, automation, and AI agent must respect.

It is not Terms of Service, a Privacy Policy, a compliance manual, a contract, or a statement of any country's law. It does not give legal advice, and it does not define operational legal procedures. It defines only the timeless legal constraints within which Open Lance operates.

As a document, this sits at the Legal authority level defined in knowledge/README.md. It must remain consistent with the higher-authority documents above it, and every lower-authority document must respect it. Applicable law itself is an absolute external constraint that no Open Lance document or decision may override.

Open Lance keeps its foundational responsibilities in separate documents, each with a single responsibility. This document does not restate their content; it references them.

- knowledge/company/company.md defines who Open Lance is.

# Core Legal Principles

The principles below are timeless legal principles appropriate for a global technology marketplace. They are not tied to any jurisdiction, they are not legal advice, and they contain no implementation or operational guidance. Each is described by its purpose, meaning, decision implications, escalation guidance, and canonical references.

## Respect for applicable law

- Purpose. Operating lawfully wherever Open Lance operates is the foundation of every other legal principle.
- Meaning. No goal, decision, policy, process, or automation may justify violating applicable law. Where the law applies, it prevails.
- Decision implications. When a choice may be unlawful, it is not taken until its lawfulness is confirmed by an accountable human.
- Escalation guidance. If a choice may violate the law, or its legality is uncertain, halt and escalate for human legal review.
- Canonical references. knowledge/company/principles.md (law as an absolute constraint); knowledge/company/mission.md (the mission does not override legal obligations).

## Good faith and fair dealing

- Purpose. Trust in the marketplace depends on honest and fair conduct in every legal relationship.
- Meaning. Act honestly and fairly toward participants, partners, and authorities, and do not deceive, mislead, or exploit a legal advantage in bad faith.
- Decision implications. Prefer the option a reasonable party would consider honest and fair, and reject options that depend on bad-faith conduct.
- Escalation guidance. If an option appears technically permissible but deceptive or unfair, escalate rather than proceed.
- Canonical references. knowledge/company/company.md (fairness, transparency); knowledge/company/principles.md (choose transparency and honesty).

## Honor contractual and binding obligations

- Purpose. Commitments must be reliable for the marketplace to function.
- Meaning. Respect the agreements, terms, and commitments that Open Lance and its participants enter into, and do not ignore or evade a binding obligation.
- Decision implications. Confirm an action is consistent with existing obligations before taking it. Any change to an obligation is made openly and lawfully, never by circumvention.
- Escalation guidance. If an action would breach or conflict with a binding obligation, halt and escalate.
- Canonical references. knowledge/company/principles.md (serve both sides fairly); the legal instruments under knowledge/legal/ that define specific terms.

## Respect intellectual property and the rights of others

- Purpose. A lawful marketplace protects the ownership and rights of everyone in it.
- Meaning. Respect intellectual property, ownership, confidentiality, and the legal rights of participants and third parties, and do not infringe, misappropriate, or induce others to.
- Decision implications. Confirm the right to use or publish material before doing so. When the right is unclear, do not proceed.
- Escalation guidance. If the right to content, data, or materials is uncertain, halt and escalate.
- Canonical references. knowledge/company/principles.md (respect participant privacy); knowledge/company/mission.md (fairness to both sides).

## Lawful handling of information and respect for user rights

- Purpose. Participants entrust Open Lance with information and retain rights over it.
- Meaning. Handle personal and confidential information lawfully, for legitimate purposes only, and respect the legal rights participants hold over their information.
- Decision implications. Collect, use, or share information only where there is a lawful and legitimate basis, and prefer the option that best respects participant rights.
- Escalation guidance. If a use of information may exceed what is lawful or legitimately expected, halt and escalate.
- Canonical references. knowledge/company/principles.md (respect participant privacy, protect security and safety); the data and privacy documents under knowledge/legal/.

## Fair and non-discriminatory treatment

- Purpose. The law and the marketplace both require even-handed treatment.
- Meaning. Treat participants fairly and consistently, and do not treat them unlawfully or discriminate on unlawful grounds.
- Decision implications. Prefer options that apply consistent, defensible criteria, and reject options that would treat similar participants differently without a lawful basis.
- Escalation guidance. If a decision could amount to unlawful or unfair treatment, halt and escalate.
- Canonical references. knowledge/company/company.md (fairness); knowledge/company/principles.md (serve both sides fairly).

## Record integrity and truthfulness

- Purpose. Reliable records are the basis of accountability, trust, and lawful operation.
- Meaning. Keep accurate and complete records of legally significant events, and never falsify, backdate, or destroy records to obscure the truth.
- Decision implications. Prefer options that preserve an accurate and verifiable record, and never choose an option that depends on altering or hiding one.
- Escalation guidance. If preserving an accurate record conflicts with another goal, halt and escalate.
- Canonical references. knowledge/company/principles.md (choose transparency and honesty, protect security and safety).

## Human accountability for legal responsibility

- Purpose. Legal responsibility rests with people, not tools.
- Meaning. Automation and AI do not absolve Open Lance of legal responsibility and do not replace accountable human judgment on legal matters. A human is accountable for legally significant decisions.
- Decision implications. Legally significant decisions are made or confirmed by an accountable human. Automation may assist, but it does not decide them alone.
- Escalation guidance. If an automated or AI action would make a legally significant decision without human accountability, halt and escalate.
- Canonical references. knowledge/README.md (human stewardship and governance); knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md (human approval and accountability).

## Transparency and cooperation with lawful authority

- Purpose. Lawful operation requires honesty toward those with a legitimate right to information.
- Meaning. Be transparent and cooperative with participants, partners, and authorities where the law or a legitimate obligation requires, and do not conceal, obstruct, or misrepresent.
- Decision implications. Prefer disclosure where disclosure is legally owed, and never choose concealment to evade a lawful obligation.
- Escalation guidance. If an obligation to disclose or cooperate is unclear, or is in tension with another duty, halt and escalate.
- Canonical references. knowledge/company/principles.md (choose transparency and honesty).

# AI Legal Constraints

These constraints govern how AI contributors behave when legal matters or legal uncertainty are involved. They describe behavior, not workflows or compliance procedures.

- AI agents do not provide legal advice and do not present themselves as a legal authority.
- AI agents do not create, change, waive, or interpret binding legal obligations or terms. Those belong to accountable humans and to the legal documents that own them.
- When a task has legal implications, or the lawfulness of an action is uncertain, an AI agent halts and escalates for human legal review rather than deciding the legal question itself.
- AI agents treat applicable law and these legal principles as absolute constraints, and never take an action they have reason to believe may be unlawful.
- AI agents preserve record integrity. They do not falsify, alter, backdate, or delete records that may have legal significance.
- AI agents default to caution. When unsure whether something is lawful or legally permissible, they treat it as requiring human review.
- AI agents follow the escalation process defined in knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md. This document does not define legal workflows or compliance procedures.
