# Open Lance Data Retention Policy

This document owns Open Lance's enduring data retention policy: the durable governance of how long information is kept and when it is removed. It is a legal policy in the Legal namespace and follows the Legal Document Standard defined in knowledge/legal/README.md. It instantiates the foundational legal principles of lawful handling of information and of record integrity owned by knowledge/company/legal.md, and it conforms to them and never restates or weakens them. Where this policy and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This policy owns the durable retention policy only. It is not a retention schedule, it is not legal advice, it names no jurisdiction's law, and it specifies no implementation.

# Purpose

This document owns one legal concern: the enduring policy governing how long Open Lance keeps information and when it removes it. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine the durable retention policy, distinct from how information is handled while held, from the systems that carry retention out, and from the law of any jurisdiction.

# Principles

These are the enduring, domain-specific principles Open Lance holds for retention. Each instantiates a foundational legal principle owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and applies it to this concern.

- Retention serves a purpose. Information is kept only for defined, legitimate purposes, and not simply because it was collected.
- Retention is time-limited. Information is kept only as long as those purposes, or a legitimate legal obligation, require.
- Removal follows the purpose. When the purpose for holding information ends and no obligation requires keeping it, the information is removed or anonymized.
- Records of legal significance are preserved. Where record integrity or a legal obligation requires keeping a record, it is kept accurately and is not destroyed to obscure the truth, applying the principle of record integrity and truthfulness.
- Retention respects participant rights. Retention is consistent with the rights participants hold over their information, owned by knowledge/legal/privacy.md.

# Requirements

These durable requirements follow from the principles above. They contain no jurisdiction-specific law and no implementation.

- Every category of information has a legitimate purpose that justifies keeping it, and is not kept without one.
- Information is removed or anonymized once its purpose has ended and no legitimate obligation requires its continued retention.
- Where a legal obligation or record-integrity need requires retention beyond the operational purpose, that retention is limited to what the obligation requires.
- A participant's request concerning removal of their information is honored consistent with the rights owned by knowledge/legal/privacy.md, subject only to obligations that lawfully require retention.
- The specific durations, schedules, and mechanisms that carry retention out are owned by the systems that implement them, not by this policy.

# Responsibilities

These responsibilities are assigned by role. The roles themselves are owned by knowledge/product/roles.md and are referenced here, not defined.

- Open Lance is responsible for retaining information only for legitimate purposes, for removing it when those purposes end, and for preserving records that must be preserved.
- Participants, in every role defined by knowledge/product/roles.md, are responsible for exercising their rights concerning their information through the means Open Lance provides.
- Accountable humans are responsible for decisions to retain or remove information that carry legal significance, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.

# Boundaries

This document owns the retention policy only. It owns none of the following, and references each by its canonical owner.

- The foundational information-handling and record-integrity principles: knowledge/company/legal.md.
- How information is handled, protected, and used while held: knowledge/legal/privacy.md.
- The retention durations, schedules, and mechanisms, and any operational retention process: the codebase, operational systems, and knowledge/product/.
- Applicable law and legal advice: an absolute external constraint and the province of accountable humans and qualified counsel; the specific retention requirements that any jurisdiction imposes are identified and interpreted by them, not by this policy.
