# Open Lance Search Strategy

This document owns the Search strategy for Open Lance: how it becomes discoverable when people search. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy only. Specific keywords, tags, metadata, and tools are implementation owned by operational systems and the codebase. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic decision about how Open Lance earns discoverability in search. Its purpose is that each audience can find Open Lance when it searches for what Open Lance offers, and that searches for the brand resolve to Open Lance.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is that search becomes a durable, compounding source of the right audiences, and that the brand is not lost to unrelated entities that share its name. This serves acquisition in the growth lifecycle owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.

# Strategy

- Own the brand name in search. Because the Open Lance name collides with unrelated entities, as recorded in knowledge/company/company.md, a durable priority is that searches for the brand resolve to Open Lance and its operating company, and are not confused with those unrelated entities. This document references that collision rather than restating it.
- Be discoverable for real intent. Open Lance aims to be found for the needs each audience actually searches on, expressed in the audience's own language owned by knowledge/customers/, and grounded in offerings owned by knowledge/product/.
- Earn discoverability through usefulness. Search visibility is earned through genuinely useful content, owned by knowledge/marketing/content-strategy.md, and honest representation, never through manipulation.
- Stay technology-neutral. This strategy owns the durable approach, to be findable, to own the brand name, and to match real search intent. It does not own specific keywords, tags, metadata, structured data, or tools, which are implementation owned by operational systems and the codebase.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- The brand identity and the recorded name collisions: knowledge/company/company.md.

# Application

This strategy directs what Open Lance aims to be found for and how it protects its brand name in search, guiding the content and site accordingly. The specific keywords, tags, metadata, and tooling that implement it are operational and live outside the knowledge repository. This document creates no page or tag.
