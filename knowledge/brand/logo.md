# Open Lance Logo Standard

This document owns the Logo standard for Open Lance: what the logo is, how it holds together, and how it is used correctly. It is a brand standard document within the Brand namespace, and it follows the Brand Document Standard defined in knowledge/brand/README.md. This document owns the standard only. The color of the logo is owned by knowledge/brand/color.md, the typeface of its wordmark by knowledge/brand/typography.md, the organization's name by knowledge/company/company.md, and every logo file and asset by the asset systems. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the standard for the Open Lance logo. Its purpose is that the logo is always recognizable, legible, and undistorted wherever it appears, so it can carry the brand's identity in a single, stable mark.

# Standard

The Open Lance logo holds to the following durable rules.

- The logo has two parts: a symbol and a wordmark. The symbol is a single, balanced geometric hexagon form, simple enough to stay recognizable at small sizes. The wordmark is the organization's name, set in the brand's primary typeface.
- The parts are used together as the primary form. The symbol may stand alone where space or context requires a compact mark; the wordmark alone is used only where the symbol cannot be.
- Color follows the color standard. The symbol carries the brand's primary color, and the wordmark uses the primary text neutral, as owned by knowledge/brand/color.md. A single-color version exists for constrained or low-contrast contexts.
- Clear space and minimum size are always respected. The logo is given uncrowded space around it and is never shown so small that it loses legibility.
- Integrity is absolute. The logo is never stretched, distorted, rotated, recolored outside the sanctioned palette, outlined, shadowed, re-proportioned, or redrawn, and its typeface is never substituted.
- Contrast is always sufficient. The logo is placed only on backgrounds that keep it clearly legible.

# Rationale

The logo is the most compressed expression of the brand, so it must carry the organization's character in the smallest possible space. A simple, balanced geometric mark reads as stable and trustworthy, which fits a marketplace built on secure, escrow-backed exchange, owned by knowledge/company/mission.md. Pairing that mark with a plainly set wordmark expresses the plainness and clarity the organization commits to in knowledge/company/principles.md. Strict integrity rules keep the mark constant, which is how a logo becomes recognized over time.

# Application

The logo standard applies wherever the logo appears, in every medium and at every size: screens, print, presentations, social profiles, and application icons. When placing the logo, a contributor or agent uses a sanctioned form, respects clear space and minimum size, checks contrast, and never alters the mark. This document states the standard; the actual logo files, formats, and export assets are produced and stored in the asset systems, and it never itself produces an Output.

# Boundaries

This document owns the logo standard only. It owns none of the following.

- The identity of the Logo concept in the brand inventory: knowledge/brand/brand.md.
- The brand's primary color and neutrals: knowledge/brand/color.md.
- The typeface of the wordmark: knowledge/brand/typography.md.
- The organization's name: knowledge/company/company.md.
- Imagery and iconography beyond the logo: knowledge/brand/imagery.md.
- Marketing and campaign use decisions: the Marketing namespace, knowledge/marketing/.
- Logo files, formats, exports, and asset management: the asset systems, not the knowledge repository.
- Rendering, markup, and tooling: the codebase, not the knowledge repository.
