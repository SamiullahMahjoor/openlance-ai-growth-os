# Open Lance Value Propositions Strategy

This document owns the Value Propositions strategy for Open Lance: which value it promises to each audience and why it matters to them. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy only. The facts and capabilities behind every value promise are owned by knowledge/product/ and knowledge/company/ and are referenced, not restated. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic decision about what value Open Lance leads with for each audience. Its purpose is that the market hears the value that matters most to it, grounded in what Open Lance genuinely delivers.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is that each audience quickly understands why Open Lance is worth choosing, in terms of the outcome it cares about, so that acquisition and conversion are driven by real, relevant value. This serves the company's mission, owned by knowledge/company/company.md.

# Strategy

- Lead each audience with the value that matters most to it. Businesses are led with confidence and protection in getting work done well; freelancers are led with fair access to work and reliable, fair payment. The needs behind these are owned by knowledge/customers/.
- Ground every value promise in a real capability or fact. A promise is made only where a capability owned by knowledge/product/ or a fact owned by knowledge/company/ delivers it. Value is never promised beyond what Open Lance provides.
- Prioritize value by audience and by lifecycle stage. The headline value carries acquisition; the ongoing value carries retention and growth. The lifecycle is owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.
- Keep every promise honest and provable, consistent with the messaging standard owned by knowledge/brand/messaging.md. Value propositions state the outcome; they never inflate it.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- The capabilities and pricing that deliver value: knowledge/product/features.md and knowledge/product/pricing.md.
- The company mission the value serves: knowledge/company/company.md.
- The needs of each audience: knowledge/customers/businesses.md and knowledge/customers/freelancers.md.
- The position the value expresses: knowledge/marketing/positioning.md.

# Application

The value propositions feed the messaging framework, content, channels, and campaigns, each of which expresses the value appropriate to its audience and stage. Every promise is checked against the capability that delivers it before use. This document states the value strategy in principle; it produces no headline or page, which are Outputs.

# Boundaries

This document owns the value propositions strategy only. It owns none of the following.

- The product capabilities and pricing that deliver value: knowledge/product/.
- Company facts and mission: knowledge/company/.
- Customer needs and behaviors: the Customers namespace, knowledge/customers/.
- The market position the value expresses: knowledge/marketing/positioning.md.
- The differences from alternatives: knowledge/marketing/differentiators.md.
- How value is worded: the Brand namespace, knowledge/brand/.
- Produced content: the Output level and the asset systems.
