# Open Lance Email Marketing Strategy

This document owns the Email Marketing strategy for Open Lance: how it educates, nurtures, and retains through email. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy only. Transactional and lifecycle notifications are product events owned by knowledge/product/, and email tools and templates are operational. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic decision about how Open Lance uses email to deepen the relationship over time. Its purpose is that email consistently educates, nurtures, and retains both audiences.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is that email carries an audience from interest to lasting, expanding use, by teaching and reminding at the right moments, rather than by interrupting. This serves the educate, retain, and grow stages of the lifecycle owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.

# Strategy

- Use email to educate, nurture, and retain across the lifecycle. Email carries the educate, retain, and grow stages owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md, for each audience owned by knowledge/customers/.
- Own marketing email, not transactional notification. This strategy owns nurture, education, re-engagement, and newsletter email. The transactional and lifecycle notifications that a product event triggers, such as an account, milestone, or payment notice, are the Notification concept owned by knowledge/product/ and are not marketing.
- Carry the message and the brand. Email expresses the messages owned by knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md, in the voice and tone owned by knowledge/brand/, which it applies and never redefines.
- Respect consent and value. Email is sent to those who want it and earns its place by being useful, never by pressure or volume, consistent with the honesty the brand commits to.
- Stay technology-neutral. This strategy owns the durable role and the principle of the program. The specific tool, provider, template, and cadence are operational and live outside the knowledge repository.

# Application

This strategy directs what email marketing is for and which lifecycle stages it serves. The specific programs, templates, providers, and cadences that implement it are operational, and transactional notifications are handled by the product. This document sends no email and writes no template.

# Boundaries

This document owns the email marketing strategy only. It owns none of the following.

- Transactional and lifecycle notifications triggered by product events: the Product namespace, knowledge/product/, and the process documents under knowledge/processes/.
- Voice and tone: the Brand namespace, knowledge/brand/.
- The audiences: the Customers namespace, knowledge/customers/.
- The messages: knowledge/marketing/messaging-framework.md.
- Email tools, providers, templates, and cadences: operational systems, not the knowledge repository.
- Produced emails: the Output level and the asset systems.
