# Open Lance Pricing Policy

This document is the single canonical owner of Open Lance pricing. It owns the enduring pricing policy: the philosophy, principles, structure, governance, and evolution of what Open Lance charges. After this document exists, no other document in the repository owns pricing; every other document references it.

It is a product document and follows the Product Namespace Guide defined in knowledge/product/README.md and the repository constitution in knowledge/README.md. It declares the Policy authority level, because pricing is a normative standing decision rather than a descriptive fact. It applies the higher-authority company documents and never restates them, and it conforms to the legal principles owned by knowledge/company/legal.md. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This document owns pricing policy only. It states no specific price, fee, percentage, plan, or commercial value; those are implementation, maintained in the codebase and configuration under this policy. It owns no subscription entity, no business rule, no feature, no legal obligation, and no financial reporting.

# Purpose

This document owns exactly one thing: pricing. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine, from one place, the enduring policy that governs what Open Lance charges and how that pricing is decided, kept transparent, governed, and evolved.

It answers "what is charged, and under what policy," at the level of durable philosophy and principle. It does not answer "what is being subscribed to," which is a separate architectural concern owned by knowledge/product/subscriptions.md, and it does not answer "what the current numbers are," which is implementation.

# Pricing Principles

These are the enduring principles that govern Open Lance pricing. Each is derived from the higher-authority company documents named beside it and applies them to pricing; none restates its source.

- Transparent pricing. The charges that apply are disclosed plainly and are never hidden. This serves the vision owned by knowledge/company/vision.md, and the commitment to disclose fees and pricing is owned by knowledge/legal/disclosures.md.
- Fair pricing to both sides. Pricing treats businesses and freelancers fairly and does not exploit either side, consistent with the principles owned by knowledge/company/principles.md and the mission owned by knowledge/company/mission.md.
- Understandable and predictable pricing. Pricing is simple enough to understand and predictable enough to plan around, reflecting the mission's commitment to remove unnecessary complexity.
- Pricing reflects value. What Open Lance charges corresponds to the value participants receive, rather than to what can be extracted.
- Pricing sustains the marketplace. Pricing earns the revenue that keeps the marketplace healthy and independent, as the monetization philosophy owned by knowledge/company/vision.md requires, and it favors sustainable revenue over exploitative extraction.
- No pay-to-win in pricing. Paying more never buys ranking, placement, or an unfair advantage in the marketplace; participants compete on reputation and fit, not on budget. This applies the marketplace philosophy owned by knowledge/company/company.md.
- Pricing is explainable and consistent. Every charge can be explained, and pricing presented anywhere is consistent with this single canonical source.
- Pricing ownership is explicit. Pricing has exactly one owner, this document, so that pricing is never scattered, duplicated, or invented.
- Pricing respects the law and the principles. Pricing conforms to the legal principles owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and the legal policies under knowledge/legal/, and never overrides them.

# Pricing Structure

This section names the categories of pricing that Open Lance governs and the policy for each. It states no value. Every specific number, rate, tier, or amount is implementation, maintained in the codebase and configuration under this policy. Every subscription entity referenced here is owned by knowledge/product/subscriptions.md, and its lifecycle by the documents named in the Boundaries section.

- Marketplace fee. Open Lance applies a platform fee to marketplace transactions. That a fee is derived at all is recorded by the Fee Calculation business rule, owned by knowledge/product/business-rules.md; this document owns the policy for that fee, and its values are implementation.
- Commission policy. Where Open Lance takes a commission on marketplace earnings, the policy governing that commission is owned here; its values are implementation.
- Withdrawal fee. The policy for any fee applied when a participant withdraws funds is owned here; the withdrawal activity is executed by knowledge/processes/withdrawal.md, and any fee is derived under the Fee Calculation business rule.
- Processing fee. The policy for any cost of payment processing that Open Lance passes on is owned here; the processing mechanism and any processor are implementation, owned by the codebase.
- Subscription pricing. The pricing, tiers, and commercial terms of memberships are owned here. What a subscription is, and its lifecycle, are owned by knowledge/product/subscriptions.md and are not owned here.
- Bid-pack pricing. The pricing and terms of purchasable proposal capacity are owned here. What a bid pack is, as an entity, is owned by knowledge/product/entities.md and knowledge/product/subscriptions.md and is not owned here.
- Service and engagement pricing. The framework by which productized services and engagements are priced is owned here. The amounts that providers and parties set for their own work are set by them, and Open Lance applies its own charges under this policy; this document owns the framework, not the party-set amounts.
- Discount, promotion, trial, founding-member, and lifetime pricing. The enduring policy and principles for these are owned here, including that they are used deliberately and in service of attracting participants who want the product rather than only a lower price, consistent with knowledge/company/company.md. The specific offers, durations, and values are implementation under this policy.

# Pricing Governance

Pricing is governed, not improvised.

- Single canonical ownership. This document is the only owner of pricing. Any pricing knowledge elsewhere is a reference to this document, never a second source.
- Human accountability. Pricing decisions are made and approved by accountable humans. Automation and AI apply pricing and present it; they never set it, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.
- One canonical source in use. All pricing that is presented anywhere draws on this policy and the values it governs. Agents present pricing as current and never invent a price, fee, percentage, plan, or value, consistent with knowledge/company/company.md.
- Conformance. Pricing conforms to the legal principles and policies owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and knowledge/legal/, and to the mission, vision, and principles owned by knowledge/company/. Where a conflict arises, the higher-authority document governs.
- Values live in implementation. The specific numeric values of prices, fees, commissions, and offers are maintained in the codebase and configuration under this policy, and are not recorded in this document.

# Pricing Evolution

Pricing changes over time; the policy that governs it endures.

- Durable policy, changeable values. The pricing philosophy, principles, and governance in this document are durable. Specific prices, fees, and offers are expected to change, and they change in implementation under this policy without changing the policy.
- Pricing is current, not permanent. Pricing is always presented as the current state rather than a permanent guarantee, consistent with knowledge/company/company.md.
- Deliberate and governed change. Changes to pricing are deliberate, follow the amendment process owned by knowledge/CONTRIBUTING.md, and are made by accountable humans.
- Communicated and disclosed. Material pricing changes are communicated to affected participants and disclosed, consistent with the disclosure policy owned by knowledge/legal/disclosures.md and the fair-treatment policy owned by knowledge/legal/consumer-protection.md.
- Additive growth. As Open Lance genuinely introduces a new category of pricing, it is added to this document. The structure of this document does not change as pricing grows, and pricing is never moved out to another owner.
