# Open Lance Consumer Protection Policy

This document owns Open Lance's enduring consumer protection policy: the durable governance of fair treatment and protection of those who use the platform. It is a legal policy in the Legal namespace and follows the Legal Document Standard defined in knowledge/legal/README.md. It instantiates the foundational legal principles of good faith and fair dealing, and of fair and non-discriminatory treatment, owned by knowledge/company/legal.md, and it conforms to them and never restates or weakens them. Where this policy and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This policy owns the durable consumer-protection policy only. It is not a protection mechanism, it is not a dispute procedure, it is not legal advice, and it names no jurisdiction's law.

# Purpose

This document owns one legal concern: the enduring policy governing how Open Lance treats and protects the people who use it. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine the durable consumer-protection policy, distinct from the mechanisms that deliver protection, from the procedures that resolve disputes, and from the law of any jurisdiction. It expresses, in legal terms, the fairness commitment that runs through Open Lance's mission and principles, which are owned by the Company namespace.

# Principles

These are the enduring, domain-specific principles Open Lance holds for consumer protection. Each instantiates a foundational legal principle owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and applies it to this concern.

- Fair and honest treatment. Participants are treated fairly and honestly, and are not deceived, misled, or exploited.
- No manipulation. Open Lance does not use deceptive or manipulative practices, hidden terms, or pressure that undermines a participant's ability to decide freely.
- Protection against loss. Open Lance provides protections that guard participants against loss in their dealings on the platform, with the mechanisms that deliver those protections owned by knowledge/product/ and knowledge/processes/.
- Fair and accessible redress. When something goes wrong, participants have access to fair and workable redress, with the resolution procedures owned by the process documents under knowledge/processes/.
- Consistent treatment. Similar participants are treated consistently, and not differently without a fair and defensible basis.

# Requirements

These durable requirements follow from the principles above. They contain no jurisdiction-specific law and no implementation.

- Participants are dealt with honestly and fairly, and deceptive or manipulative practices, hidden material terms, and undue pressure are prohibited.
- Material terms, fees, and conditions that affect participants are made clear before they commit, consistent with the disclosure policy owned by knowledge/legal/disclosures.md.
- Protections that guard participants against loss are provided and honored, with the specific mechanisms, such as safeguarding of funds in an engagement, owned by knowledge/product/ and executed through knowledge/processes/.
- Participants have access to fair, workable redress when a dealing goes wrong, with the dispute and resolution procedures owned by knowledge/processes/.
- Participants in similar situations are treated consistently, without unfair or unlawful differences in treatment.

# Responsibilities

These responsibilities are assigned by role. The roles themselves are owned by knowledge/product/roles.md and are referenced here, not defined.

- Open Lance is responsible for dealing fairly and honestly, for providing and honoring participant protections, for making material terms clear, and for providing fair redress.
- Participants, in every role defined by knowledge/product/roles.md, are responsible for acting in good faith toward one another and toward the platform.
- Accountable humans are responsible for protection and redress decisions that carry legal significance, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.

# Boundaries

This document owns the consumer-protection policy only. It owns none of the following, and references each by its canonical owner.

- The foundational fair-dealing and non-discrimination principles: knowledge/company/legal.md.
- The mission and principles that express Open Lance's fairness commitment: the Company namespace.
- The protection mechanisms, such as safeguarding of funds, and the states and rules that govern them: knowledge/product/.
- The dispute and resolution procedures that deliver redress: the process documents under knowledge/processes/.
- The terms of use and the disclosure of material terms: knowledge/legal/terms.md and knowledge/legal/disclosures.md.
- The honesty standard in communication: the Brand namespace.
- Applicable law and legal advice: an absolute external constraint and the province of accountable humans and qualified counsel; the specific consumer requirements that any jurisdiction imposes are identified and interpreted by them, not by this policy.
