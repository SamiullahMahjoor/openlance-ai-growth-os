# Open Lance Differentiators Strategy

This document owns the Differentiators strategy for Open Lance: which differences from the alternatives it emphasizes in the market. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy only. The product facts and the competitor records behind every difference are owned by knowledge/product/ and knowledge/competitors/ and are referenced, not restated. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic decision about which differences Open Lance emphasizes to stand apart from alternatives. Its purpose is that the market understands why Open Lance is different in kind, not just another marketplace.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is that a prospective customer, weighing Open Lance against the alternatives, understands the specific, durable reasons it is different, so the choice is clear. This serves the position owned by knowledge/marketing/positioning.md.

# Strategy

- Emphasize durable, structural differences, not transient feature gaps. The differences Open Lance leads on are the enduring ones grounded in how it works, owned by knowledge/product/, such as protected payment, fairness to both sides, and the absence of bidding-war and pay-to-win dynamics.
- Emphasize only differences that are true, durable, and material to the audience. A difference is emphasized only where a fact owned by knowledge/product/ or knowledge/company/ supports it and it matters to the audience owned by knowledge/customers/.
- Draw differences from the intersection of what Open Lance genuinely does and what the alternatives do. The alternatives are owned by knowledge/competitors/; this strategy selects which contrasts to emphasize, and never restates or disparages a competitor.
- Keep the emphasis neutral and factual. A difference is stated as fact; competitor descriptions remain owned by knowledge/competitors/, and any in-market comparison is expressed through the messaging framework and within the brand standards.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- The capabilities and model that create the differences: knowledge/product/features.md.
- The alternatives the differences are drawn against: knowledge/competitors/competitors.md.
- The audience the differences must matter to: knowledge/customers/customers.md.
- The position the differences support: knowledge/marketing/positioning.md.

# Application

The differentiators inform the messaging framework, comparison-oriented content, and campaigns, each of which expresses a difference grounded in a real fact. Every emphasized difference is checked against the product fact that creates it and the competitor set it stands against. This document states the differentiation strategy in principle; it produces no comparison page or asset, which are Outputs.

# Boundaries

This document owns the differentiators strategy only. It owns none of the following.

- Product capabilities and the model behind the differences: knowledge/product/.
- Competitor identities and records: the Competitors namespace, knowledge/competitors/.
- The market position: knowledge/marketing/positioning.md.
- The promises of value: knowledge/marketing/value-propositions.md.
- How differences are worded and any comparison is voiced: the Brand namespace, knowledge/brand/.
- Produced comparison content: the Output level and the asset systems.
