# Open Lance Tone Standard

This document owns the Tone standard for Open Lance: how the organization's voice adjusts to context, audience, and moment while staying the same voice. It is a brand standard document within the Brand namespace, and it follows the Brand Document Standard defined in knowledge/brand/README.md. The definition of the term Tone is owned by knowledge/brand/README.md, and the voice that tone adjusts is owned by knowledge/brand/voice.md; this document owns the tone standard only. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the standard for adjusting Open Lance's voice to the situation. Its purpose is that communication feels right for its moment, energetic in an invitation, calm at a payment, empathetic in a problem, without ever stopping sounding like Open Lance.

# Standard

Tone adjusts the voice within its fixed bounds. The voice traits owned by knowledge/brand/voice.md always hold; tone changes only the warmth, energy, and formality dialed on top of them.

- Tone never overrides the voice. It may make the voice warmer, calmer, or more energetic, but it never makes it boastful, evasive, cold, or unfair.
- Invitation and discovery. When inviting someone to act or explore, the tone is energetic, confident, and encouraging.
- Transaction and money. When money, escrow, or a commitment is involved, the tone is calm, precise, and reassuring. Certainty matters more than energy here.
- Problems, errors, and disputes. When something has gone wrong, the tone is empathetic, plain, and accountable. It acknowledges the situation, states what happens next, and never blames the reader.
- Trust, safety, and obligations. When explaining rules, risks, or requirements, the tone is plain and exact, informative without alarm.
- Milestones and success. When marking a good outcome, the tone is warm and brief. It celebrates the reader's progress without inflating it.

When two situations meet, the more serious situation sets the tone. Money and problems always take precedence over energy.

# Rationale

A single flat tone cannot serve a marketplace where people invite, transact, resolve conflict, and celebrate in the same day. The organization's principles, owned by knowledge/company/principles.md, require that trust and user safety come before growth, so the tone turns calm and careful exactly where stakes are highest, and turns energetic only where the stakes are low. Adjusting tone while holding the voice keeps the organization recognizable and appropriate at once, which is what a transparent and fair marketplace, defined in knowledge/company/mission.md, owes its users.

# Application

Tone applies to every outward communication, in every medium, and is chosen by the situation, not the channel. The same page may shift tone between an invitation and a payment step. Before publishing, a writer or agent identifies the situation, applies the matching tone, and confirms the voice traits still hold. This document states the standard in principle; it never writes a specific piece of content, which is an Output.
