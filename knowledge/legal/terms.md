# Open Lance Terms of Use Policy

This document owns Open Lance's enduring Terms of Use policy: the durable governance of the relationship between Open Lance and those who use it. It is a legal policy in the Legal namespace and follows the Legal Document Standard defined in knowledge/legal/README.md. It instantiates the foundational legal principles owned by knowledge/company/legal.md, and it conforms to them and never restates or weakens them. Where this policy and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This policy owns the durable terms-of-use policy only. It is not the produced Terms of Service instrument, it is not legal advice, and it names no jurisdiction's law.

# Purpose

This document owns one legal concern: the enduring policy governing the relationship between Open Lance and the participants who use it. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine the durable policy behind that relationship, independent of the specific instrument that expresses it or the law of any jurisdiction.

# Principles

These are the enduring, domain-specific principles Open Lance holds for the terms of use. Each instantiates a foundational legal principle owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and applies it to this concern.

- Use is governed by terms. Participation in Open Lance takes place under terms that both the platform and the participant accept. This applies the principle of honoring contractual and binding obligations.
- The relationship is mutual and defined. The terms set out the rights and responsibilities of the platform and of participants, so each side knows what it may expect and what it owes.
- The terms are fair and honest. They are written to be understood, do not conceal material obligations, and reflect the good faith and fair dealing that the marketplace depends on.
- The terms are honored. Open Lance and participants keep the commitments the terms establish, and neither side evades a binding obligation by circumvention.
- Changes are open and lawful. The terms may change over time, but material changes are made openly, communicated, and never applied to evade an existing obligation.

# Requirements

These durable requirements follow from the principles above. They contain no jurisdiction-specific law and no implementation.

- Participation requires acceptance of the terms that govern it, and the point at which acceptance occurs is clear to the participant.
- The terms state the rights and responsibilities of the platform and of participants, and the conduct expected of participants, deferring the detail of that conduct to knowledge/legal/acceptable-use.md.
- The terms are consistent with the mission and principles of Open Lance, including its commitment to fairness, and never contradict knowledge/company/legal.md.
- Material changes to the terms are communicated to affected participants, and continued participation after a change is not treated as acceptance of a change that was not made clear.
- The terms reference, and do not restate, the policies that own related concerns, including privacy, intellectual property, content ownership, and pricing.

# Responsibilities

These responsibilities are assigned by role. The roles themselves are owned by knowledge/product/roles.md and are referenced here, not defined.

- Open Lance is responsible for providing terms that are clear, fair, and consistent with its principles, for communicating material changes, and for honoring the commitments the terms establish.
- Participants who use the platform, in every role defined by knowledge/product/roles.md, are responsible for accepting the terms that govern their use and for abiding by them.
- Accountable humans are responsible for approving the terms and any change to them, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.

# Boundaries

This document owns the terms-of-use policy only. It owns none of the following, and references each by its canonical owner.

- The foundational legal principles: knowledge/company/legal.md.
- The produced Terms of Service instrument and its wording: the Output level.
- The conduct that is permitted and prohibited: knowledge/legal/acceptable-use.md.
- The handling of personal information: knowledge/legal/privacy.md.
- Intellectual property and the ownership of content and work product: knowledge/legal/intellectual-property.md and knowledge/legal/content-ownership.md.
- Fees, pricing, and commercial terms: knowledge/product/pricing.md.
- The operating entity and company identity: knowledge/company/company.md.
- Dispute execution and other procedures: the process documents under knowledge/processes/.
- Applicable law and legal advice: an absolute external constraint and the province of accountable humans and qualified counsel; the specific requirements that any jurisdiction imposes are identified and interpreted by them, not by this policy.
