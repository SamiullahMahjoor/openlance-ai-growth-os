# Open Lance Freelancers Segment

This document owns the Freelancers segment for Open Lance: the supply side of the marketplace. It is a customer document within the Customers namespace, and it follows the Customer Document Standard defined in knowledge/customers/README.md. The identity of this segment in the inventory is owned by knowledge/customers/customers.md, and the Freelancer role is owned by knowledge/product/roles.md; this document owns the segment profile only. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the durable understanding of who Open Lance's freelance customers are, what they need, how they behave, and how they speak. Its purpose is that every agent and contributor works from the same picture of the supply side, without holding any personal data and without restating what other documents own.

# Who They Are

The canonical identity of this segment is owned by knowledge/customers/customers.md. Within it, freelancers range from an individual taking on projects alone to a freelancer who grows into a team and takes on larger work together. What unites them is the supply position: they offer skills, do the work, and are paid for it. They tend to hold the Freelancer role, owned by knowledge/product/roles.md.

Working alone and working as a team are patterns within this one segment, not separate segments. The team a freelancer forms is a product entity owned by knowledge/product/entities.md; this document records the behavior of growing into one, not the entity.

# Needs

Freelancers come to Open Lance to satisfy the following durable needs. Each need is met by a product capability owned by knowledge/product/features.md; this document records the need, not the capability.

- Find real, fair work without having to outspend others just to be seen.
- Be paid reliably and promptly for work they have completed.
- Keep more of what they earn rather than losing it to heavy platform costs.
- Win work on the merit of their skill and reputation, on an equal footing.
- Build a reputation that compounds into repeat and better work.
- Grow their capacity over time, including by working as a team.

The commercial terms behind these needs are owned by knowledge/product/pricing.md, and the mechanisms that satisfy them, such as escrow, reputation, and proposals, are owned by knowledge/product/features.md and knowledge/product/entities.md. This document points to them and never restates them.

# Behaviors

Freelancers tend to decide and act in the following durable patterns. The workflows and processes named here are owned by knowledge/product/workflows.md and the process documents under knowledge/processes/; this document records the behavior, not the workflow.

- They present who they are and what they do, choose the fields they work in, and put themselves forward for work that fits.
- They invest their limited effort where they have a fair chance, and they resent systems that reward spending over merit.
- They deliver work, seek approval, and expect to be paid promptly once it is accepted.
- They build reputation deliberately, knowing it wins future work, and they value being paid on time above almost everything.
- As they grow, they may take on ongoing arrangements or form a team to handle more work.

# Language

Freelancers describe their situation in the words of finding work and getting paid, not in platform terms. They talk about finding work, gigs, or projects, working with clients, being paid on time, their rates, their portfolio, submitting proposals or bids, their reputation and reviews, and getting their earnings out. They frame success as steady, fair-paying work that respects their skill and pays reliably.

This is the audience's own language, recorded so that communication can meet it. How Open Lance itself chooses words is a separate standard owned by knowledge/brand/vocabulary.md, and this document never restates it.
