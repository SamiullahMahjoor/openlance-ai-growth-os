# Open Lance Voice Standard

This document owns the Voice standard for Open Lance: the durable character of the organization's language across every communication. It is a brand standard document within the Brand namespace, and it follows the Brand Document Standard defined in knowledge/brand/README.md. The definition of the term Voice is owned by knowledge/brand/README.md; this document owns the standard only. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the standard for how Open Lance sounds. Its purpose is that every writer and every AI agent, working in any medium, expresses the organization in one consistent, recognizable character, so that a reader can tell they are hearing Open Lance without seeing a logo.

# Standard

Open Lance's voice holds to the following durable traits in all communication.

- Plain and direct. Use short, concrete sentences. Say the thing and cut filler. Prefer common words over jargon.
- Confident, not boastful. Make specific claims that can be backed by fact. Never exaggerate, hype, or use empty superlatives.
- Fair and on the user's side. Address businesses and freelancers with equal respect, and frame the platform as removing unfair barriers rather than as a favor granted.
- Warm and human. Write to a person, not to a market. Be encouraging and calm, never cold, bureaucratic, or pushy.
- Honest and transparent. Never overpromise, obscure, or mislead. When something is a limit or a cost, state it plainly.
- Active and reader-first. Prefer the active voice and the second person, and lead with the reader's benefit.
- Respectful and inclusive. Never condescend, pressure, shame, or exclude any reader.

These traits are constant. They do not change by channel, audience, or format.

# Rationale

The voice is a direct expression of who Open Lance is. Its identity, mission, and operating principles, owned by knowledge/company/company.md, knowledge/company/mission.md, and knowledge/company/principles.md, commit the organization to transparency, fairness, security, and simplicity. A marketplace that promises those things must also sound like them: plain speech expresses simplicity, specific and honest claims express transparency, and equal respect for both sides expresses fairness. The voice makes the organization's character audible in its words.

# Application

The voice applies to every outward communication, regardless of medium: websites, applications, documentation, email, social posts, presentations, printed material, and AI-generated content. The character stays the same everywhere. Only the tone, the situational adjustment of the voice, changes with context, and tone is owned by knowledge/brand/tone.md. Before publishing any text, a writer or agent checks it against the traits above and revises anything that sounds boastful, evasive, cold, or unfair. This document states the standard in principle; it never writes or prescribes a specific piece of content, which is an Output.

# Boundaries

This document owns the voice standard only. It owns none of the following.

- Content systems, rendering, and tooling: the codebase, not the knowledge repository.
