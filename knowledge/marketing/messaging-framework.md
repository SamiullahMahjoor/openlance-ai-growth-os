# Open Lance Messaging Framework Strategy

This document owns the Messaging Framework strategy for Open Lance: what messages it conveys, to whom, and in what order. It is a marketing strategy document within the Marketing namespace, and it follows the Marketing Document Standard defined in knowledge/marketing/README.md. Its identity in the inventory is owned by knowledge/marketing/marketing.md; this document owns the strategy only. How a message is constructed, the voice, tone, and messaging standard, is owned by knowledge/brand/ and is applied, not redefined. Where this document and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs.

# Purpose

This document owns the strategic architecture of Open Lance's messages: the hierarchy of what is said, mapped to audience and lifecycle stage. Its purpose is that every communication tells one coherent story, whoever or whatever produces it.

# Strategic Intent

The intent is that the market hears a single, consistent story across every channel and campaign, built from the same strategic substance, so that repetition compounds rather than confuses. This serves the position owned by knowledge/marketing/positioning.md.

# Strategy

- The message architecture has a clear hierarchy. The core message expresses the position owned by knowledge/marketing/positioning.md; the audience messages express the value propositions owned by knowledge/marketing/value-propositions.md; the distinguishing messages express the differentiators owned by knowledge/marketing/differentiators.md.
- Every message is mapped to an audience and a lifecycle stage. Which message leads depends on whether the audience is a business or a freelancer, owned by knowledge/customers/, and on whether the stage is acquire, educate, convert, retain, or grow, owned by knowledge/marketing/growth-strategy.md.
- This framework owns what to say and when, not how to say it. How a message is worded, its voice, tone, vocabulary, and construction, is owned by knowledge/brand/messaging.md and knowledge/brand/voice.md and is applied here, never restated or overridden.
- The architecture is consistent across all channels and campaigns. Channel and campaign strategies draw their messages from this framework rather than inventing their own.

# Inputs

This strategy consumes the following frozen knowledge by reference and never restates it.

- The position, value propositions, and differentiators that supply the substance: knowledge/marketing/positioning.md, knowledge/marketing/value-propositions.md, and knowledge/marketing/differentiators.md.
- The audiences messages are mapped to: knowledge/customers/customers.md.
- The standard for how a message is constructed: knowledge/brand/messaging.md, and the voice and tone at knowledge/brand/voice.md and knowledge/brand/tone.md.

# Application

The messaging framework is the source every channel and campaign draws its messages from. Content, social, email, campaigns, and launches select the right message for their audience and stage from this framework, then construct it to the brand standard. This document states the message architecture in principle; it writes no specific message, which is an Output.

# Boundaries

This document owns the messaging framework strategy only. It owns none of the following.

- How a message is worded, its voice, tone, vocabulary, and construction standard: the Brand namespace, knowledge/brand/.
- The strategic substance the messages carry: knowledge/marketing/positioning.md, knowledge/marketing/value-propositions.md, and knowledge/marketing/differentiators.md.
- The facts messages assert: knowledge/company/ and knowledge/product/.
- The audiences and their language: the Customers namespace, knowledge/customers/.
- Produced messages and copy: the Output level and the asset systems.
