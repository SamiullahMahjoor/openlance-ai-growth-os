# Open Lance Disclosures Policy

This document owns Open Lance's enduring disclosures policy: the durable governance of what Open Lance commits to disclose. It is a legal policy in the Legal namespace and follows the Legal Document Standard defined in knowledge/legal/README.md. It instantiates the foundational legal principles of transparency and cooperation with lawful authority, and of good faith and fair dealing, owned by knowledge/company/legal.md, and it conforms to them and never restates or weakens them. Where this policy and a higher-authority document differ, the higher-authority document governs, and applicable law overrides every internal document.

This policy owns the durable disclosure policy only. It is not the disclosed facts, it is not a produced disclosure, it is not legal advice, and it names no jurisdiction's law.

# Purpose

This document owns one legal concern: the enduring policy governing what Open Lance discloses to participants and how. It exists so that any human or AI agent can determine the durable disclosure commitment, distinct from the specific facts that are disclosed, which are owned by their own documents, and from the law of any jurisdiction.

# Principles

These are the enduring, domain-specific principles Open Lance holds for disclosures. Each instantiates a foundational legal principle owned by knowledge/company/legal.md and applies it to this concern.

- Material information is disclosed. Participants are told the material information they need to make an informed decision about using the platform.
- Disclosure is honest. What is disclosed is accurate and not misleading, and material facts are not concealed to gain an advantage.
- Disclosure is clear and accessible. Disclosures are made in a way participants can find and understand, not buried or obscured.
- Disclosure is timely. Material information is disclosed when participants need it to make a decision, and material changes are disclosed as they arise.

# Requirements

These durable requirements follow from the principles above. They contain no jurisdiction-specific law and no implementation.

- Open Lance discloses the material information participants need, including who operates the platform, how the platform works, the material terms of use, how personal information is handled, and the fees and costs that apply.
- Each disclosed fact is owned by the document that owns that knowledge, and this policy governs the commitment to disclose it, not its content. The operating entity is owned by knowledge/company/company.md, the fees and pricing by knowledge/product/pricing.md, the data practices by knowledge/legal/privacy.md, and the terms by knowledge/legal/terms.md.
- Disclosures are accurate, are not misleading, and do not omit a material fact whose omission would mislead.
- Disclosures are made accessibly and in time for participants to act on them, and material changes are disclosed as they arise.

# Responsibilities

These responsibilities are assigned by role. The roles themselves are owned by knowledge/product/roles.md and are referenced here, not defined.

- Open Lance is responsible for disclosing material information honestly, clearly, accessibly, and in time, and for keeping disclosures accurate as facts change.
- Participants, in every role defined by knowledge/product/roles.md, are entitled to rely on Open Lance's disclosures and are responsible for the disclosures they in turn make to others through the platform.
- Accountable humans are responsible for disclosure decisions that carry legal significance, consistent with the human accountability owned by knowledge/company/legal.md.

# Boundaries

This document owns the disclosure policy only. It owns none of the following, and references each by its canonical owner.

- The foundational transparency and fair-dealing principles: knowledge/company/legal.md.
- The operating entity and company identity that are disclosed: knowledge/company/company.md.
- The fees, pricing, and commercial terms that are disclosed: knowledge/product/pricing.md.
- The data practices that are disclosed: knowledge/legal/privacy.md.
- The terms of use that are disclosed: knowledge/legal/terms.md.
- The produced disclosures and their wording: the Output level.
- Applicable law and legal advice: an absolute external constraint and the province of accountable humans and qualified counsel; the specific disclosure requirements that any jurisdiction imposes are identified and interpreted by them, not by this policy.
